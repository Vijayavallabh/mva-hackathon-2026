# Compare mechanisms before choosing an intervention

19 October 2026. Supersedes the compressed v38 comparison for current use. No
rescue-priority drug is established. Everolimus remains an optional mechanistic
probe, currently on HOLD for missing qualification; HCQ remains reserve. This is
an experiment-selection aid, not a treatment plan.

| Route | Favorable evidence | Limits and opposing evidence | Next discriminating question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everolimus / mTORC1 | Sieben Figure 5 reports mTORC1 substrate phosphorylation in skeletal muscle of heterozygous BubR1+/X753 mice, with relevance to later sarcopenia. Everolimus can perturb mTORC1. | No rapalog rescue was tested. The carrier genotype, tissue and endpoint differ from the retained compound-pair hypothesis. Target pharmacology is not evidence that the pathway causes this deficit; elevation could be pathogenic or adaptive. | After endogenous functional qualification, is the specified pathway abnormality reproducible and relevant in the proposed context? Missing evidence holds the branch. A valid precise negative closes the tested branch; it does not prove universal inefficacy. |
| NAD/SIRT2 / residual BUBR1 | North links SIRT2/CBP regulation of human K668 to protein stability. Genetic SIRT2 overexpression extended median lifespan preferentially in male BubR1-hypomorphic mice; females had no median-lifespan change. NMN abundance was a separate experiment. | The hypomorph retains low amounts of wild-type protein; this pair's residual competence is unknown. Mouse K243 corresponds to human K250, not a third distinct site. K243R improves selected aged-oocyte outcomes but weakens checkpoint function and promotes tumors in a somatic mouse model. SIRT2 is not BUBR1-specific. No approved-drug rescue of this pair is shown. | Does independent, expression-matched BUBR1 restoration improve relevant function? Then distinguish a specific SIRT2/precursor intervention's effects on abundance, site/context-dependent checkpoint function and injury. Do not equate niacin, NMN and genetic SIRT2 intervention. |
| HCQ reserve | Retained abundance and aneuploid-stress observations justify retaining the hypothesis. | Abundance does not establish useful function. Chromatin-timing effects prevent using HCQ as a selective autophagy control; cross-context exposure comparisons are insufficient. | Reopen only with interpretable compound-specific flux, chromosome-function and injury evidence in an independently qualified context. |
| Endogenous qualification first | Direct RNA/protein, perturbation/restoration and functional observations can distinguish deficient abundance from defective function and model failure. | No one blot resolves all routes. Mitotic index and localization are incomplete proxies; per-division outcomes still omit cells that never divide. | Start with proposed engineered hTERT-RPE1 as a qualification comparator. Measure all-enrolled fates, first-division errors, daughter output and recovery alongside residual protein and checkpoint maintenance. Availability and feasibility remain unconfirmed. |

The shared standard is relevant mechanism, interpretable intervention, useful
function, injury, exposure and scope. A more proximal mechanism deserves attention
without automatically becoming the preferred drug. Downstream mitigation can be
useful without restoring BUBR1 itself; it needs its own causal and functional tests.
