# Qualification changes from the new CRISPR campaign

Use this with [v21 biological validation](track2-validation-v21.md) and the
[v23 RNAi controls](track2-rnai-validation-v23.md). It changes what must be tested;
it supplies no treatment recommendation, clinical exposure margin or wet-lab result.

| Claim to test | Required change | Hold or stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| More CRISPR profiles establish independent target evidence | Count guide sequences, source wells, batches and cell backgrounds separately. The 31 newer BUB1B profiles use one guide ID. Use independent perturbation and restoration with endogenous protein/function measurements. | One guide, even if reproducible, cannot pass independent-guide qualification. |
| A CRISPR response represents the relevant BUB1B deficit | Separate editing stress, strong loss, residual function and survivor selection. Include a complementary perturbation method that does not rely on the same DNA-cutting event. | An unmeasured knockout fraction or unmatched deficit remains an invalid basis for transfer to the selected alleles. |
| RNAi/CRISPR agreement identifies the intended target | Compare target recovery against all assayed genes; retain both retrieval directions, raw/PRIME and the BUB1B-transcript-removal sensitivity. Confirm target restoration changes the phenotype. | Shared downstream stress or one favorable representation cannot establish on-target functional rescue. |
| Opposite expression means benefit | Record checkpoint function, first-division errors, daughter fate and useful tissue output alongside expression. Preserve drug-plus-deficit, deficit-alone and drug-alone arms. | A better expression score with worse function or injury stops advancement. Fewer abnormal surviving cells cannot stand in for healthier tissue. |
| Everolimus identity and exposure are settled | Use reference-matching chemistry, retain separate compound IDs and nominal doses, and distinguish reprocessed/reaggregated experiments from new experiments. Measure target engagement and relevant free exposure independently. | Unresolved identity, inadequate drug QC or an unknown exposure margin blocks an efficacy interpretation; none establishes biological futility by itself. |
| A cancer-cell connection transfers to non-cancer disease | Qualify a relevant non-cancer model with the intended residual-function state. Keep tumour killing in its own branch and test deficient normal-tissue injury. | No available expression score can bypass missing normal-tissue function or safety evidence. |

The favorable mTOR literature remains relevant: acute genotoxic-stress results in
human T cells argue against claiming that mTOR inhibition can only conceal damage.
They do not establish BUB1B rescue or everolimus equivalence. Conversely, editing-induced
p53 responses and incomplete rescue after Trp53 co-deletion in BubR1-deficient mouse
cortex show why response suppression is an inadequate endpoint. Reading depth and
limits are recorded in the [source review](track2-crispr-sources-v25.json).

Unknown evidence means hold. Imprecision differs from a well-powered exclusion of
meaningful benefit. Invalid assays are repaired before interpretation; failed safety
overrides apparent benefit. Any eventual all-pass result allows only further preclinical
review. The current decision remains HOLD, with no rescue-priority drug and everolimus
an optional model-qualified mechanistic probe. HCQ stays reserve.
