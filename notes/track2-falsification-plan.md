# Track 2: standing falsification objective

Owner-directed, 2026-09-19, session 46; feat-009. This is the current working
objective alongside the preserved [original specification](track2-plan.md).
The [v9 adversarial audit](track2-adversarial-v9.md),
[report](track2-report-v9.md), [ledger](track2-evidence-v9.json) and
[validation plan](track2-validation-v9.md) supply the scientific baseline.
This document changes the continuing research process; it does not add study
results, establish safety or efficacy, or replace the immutable v9 package.

## Objective

While working toward a useful Track 2 solution, **actively and extensively search
for information that could falsify or disprove the current approach**. Treat this
as critical work because the child's life is at stake. Use weaknesses, errors,
contradictions and overlooked risks to substantially improve the solution,
including abandoning or replacing a favored approach when warranted.

The aim is a better-supported, safer research proposal, not preservation of a
particular drug, mechanism, presentation or competition narrative. A conclusion
that no candidate is adequately supported is a valid outcome. Urgency increases
the need for careful evidence; it does not lower the threshold for advancement.

## Required research cycle

1. **State the claim before seeking support.** For each consequential assumption,
   record the population/model, intervention and direction, comparator, outcome,
   time scale, strongest alternative explanation, and an observation that would
   materially weaken or reject it. Separate genotype attribution, mechanism,
   functional benefit, tumour activity, exposure and safety claims. State what
   remains untestable with available evidence.
2. **Allocate substantial effort to trying to break it.** Make contrary-evidence
   retrieval a dedicated part of every substantive research cycle, before any
   candidate promotion or new release. Prioritize assumptions whose failure could
   cause harm or invalidate the proposal. A single negative-keyword query or an
   appended limitations paragraph does not meet this objective.
3. **Search broadly, then follow decisive leads deeply.** Cover the dimensions
   below, use complementary sources, trace citations backward and forward, and
   follow a credible contradiction to its primary methods and results where
   accessible. Expand beyond the favored drug and the current mechanistic story.
   Do not limit searches to papers that call their findings negative.
4. **Evaluate fairly.** Apply the same quality and relevance standards to favorable
   and unfavorable evidence. Distinguish direct contradiction, indirect concern,
   imprecision and missing evidence. Preserve positive counterweights. Check
   independence: reviews, model answers and overlapping datasets are not separate
   replications. A weak adverse paper does not prove harm; failure to find a
   contradiction does not validate the approach.
5. **Turn findings into decisions.** For every material challenge, record whether
   it changes the hypothesis, candidate disposition, assay/control, stop rule or
   next search. Correct or withdraw unsupported claims promptly. If an essential
   premise fails, stop its advancement and investigate alternatives. A citation
   or caveat alone is inadequate when the conclusion itself no longer follows.
6. **Test the revised position too.** Search for evidence against the new explanation
   or rejection, including evidence of benefit in a relevant setting. Specify
   what would justify reopening a rejected candidate. Do not replace confirmation
   bias with a preference for negative findings or move criteria after results.
7. **Leave a reproducible handoff.** Record the search, adjudication, unresolved
   high-impact questions and next discriminating action. Update current working
   decisions and, when science changes, create new versioned report/ledger/protocol/
   pitch artifacts. Preserve all earlier bound inputs and uploaded deliverables.

## Search coverage

Use public literature and allowed derived summaries only. Start without a lower
publication-date cutoff and record the actual search date and filters; include
new publications, corrections and posted results through that date. Do not equate
an English-language or accessible subset with all relevant evidence.

| Dimension | Evidence that must be actively sought |
|---|---|
| Disease and allele model | Alternative mechanisms; genotype/model mismatch; effects of residual protein, tissue and developmental stage; evidence that predictions or competition results do not resolve the biological claim. Use existing local phase audits without treating repeated absence of linkage as trans confirmation. |
| Intervention direction | Adaptive versus harmful pathway activation; feedback, dose and timing effects; opposite-direction perturbations; genetic versus pharmacological disagreement; off-target explanations. |
| Functional rescue | Failed replications and null results; cytostasis, cell death or survivor selection masquerading as rescue; protein abundance without checkpoint function; transient changes without durable tissue benefit. |
| Tumour versus constitutional outcomes | Tumour activity accompanied by normal-tissue injury; tumour-subtype and treatment-backbone mismatch; conflicting clinical trials; tumour suppression without constitutional benefit. |
| Exposure and feasibility | Unachievable or unmeasured relevant exposure, free fraction and tissue delivery, schedule dependence, assay-medium effects and clinically incompatible combinations. Keep nominal culture, plasma and whole blood, and peak/trough/AUC distinct. |
| Overlooked risks | Primary safety findings and official-label changes concerning growth, development, muscle, kidney, infection, immune function, wound healing and drug interactions where relevant. Unknown regimen, organ function or tumour subtype cannot be filled by assumption. |
| Alternatives and opportunity cost | Better-supported competing mechanisms or interventions; reasons each rejected/reserve candidate might deserve reconsideration; whether no pharmacological priority remains the soundest conclusion. |
| Source reliability | Corrections/retractions, protocol-to-publication discrepancies, registered but unpublished or discontinued studies, selective endpoints, small/unblinded experiments, overlapping cohorts and unsupported AI summaries. |

Use PubMed and Europe PMC for complementary discovery and primary-text retrieval;
trial registries and posted results for failed, stopped or unpublished studies;
and official regulators/labels for indications and safety. Add other indexes and
journal citation trails when they can close a gap. Record unavailable sources
instead of silently treating the coverage as complete.

For each drug, search its generic name, relevant synonyms, target and class with
both neutral outcome terms and contrary terms (failure, no benefit, toxicity,
resistance, impaired function, paradoxical effect, compensation). Neutral searches
are necessary because adverse findings may not appear in titles or abstracts.
Check the latest primary record and correction status before relying on a pivotal
result. Reviews and model outputs can suggest leads; verify decision-making
claims against primary evidence or official regulatory records.

## First research-cycle priorities from the v9 baseline

These are questions and planned searches, **not new findings or completed searches**.
The current disposition remains no rescue-priority drug, optional model-qualified
everolimus probe and HCQ reserve until evidence warrants a documented change.

| Priority question | Planned discovery examples | Consequence of a credible challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Could mTOR inhibition undermine BUBR1-dependent function in relevant cells? | `everolimus BUBR1`; `BUB1B mTOR inhibition checkpoint`; `rapamycin chromosome segregation`; citation trails from Goutas and Sieben. | Reassess whether even a qualified probe is informative; strengthen or terminate the relevant advancement path according to functional evidence and model relevance. |
| Could elevated signaling be compensatory in the tissue and time window of interest? | `mTORC1 inhibition muscle regeneration atrophy`; `Raptor loss developmental muscle`; neutral and contrary citation trails from the adaptive-signaling studies. | Change the directional hypothesis and assay schedule; do not use pathway suppression as a surrogate for benefit. |
| Does any apparent improvement conceal cell loss, selection or impaired later function? | `BUBR1 autophagy cell cycle survival`; `aneuploid cells rapamycin mitotic slippage`; `hydroxychloroquine BUBR1 function`. | Require all-cell fate and durable functional evidence; protein or survivor-only improvement cannot support rescue. |
| Does the exposure/safety bridge fail even if a model responds? | Compound-specific primary pharmacokinetics, tissue/free-exposure evidence, pediatric safety and official interaction records. | Keep margins unknown or reject the bridge when evidence warrants; do not infer comparability from numerically similar concentrations. |
| Are alternatives being overlooked because of the existing story? | Revisit the complete candidate ledger, failed/terminated trials, alternative target directions and independent functional studies. | Reopen candidates only with a stronger relevant evidence chain; do not promote the next name merely because a lead weakened. |

## Required record for each search and decision

Record in the dated working review (and the next ledger when decisions change):

- Claim ID, baseline/version, proposed falsifier and prior disposition.
- Exact query, source/index, date, filters and inspected result range or export;
  deduplication and inclusion/exclusion reasons. Distinguish hits, screened
  records, read studies and independent experiments.
- Primary identifier/URL, source version or public-content hash where available,
  correction status, and reading depth: abstract, selected sections, full text,
  supplement. Record access failures and what could not be checked.
- Actual model/genotype, intervention, dose/exposure matrix, duration, comparator,
  endpoint, replication unit, effect size/uncertainty when extractable, and major
  biases. Keep unreported quantities null.
- Strongest supporting and opposing interpretation, transfer limits, effect on
  confidence, revised decision/control/stop rule, and what would reverse it.
- Remaining high-impact uncertainty, next action and responsible blocker. A
  failed retrieval is not a negative result; a proposed assay is not an experiment.

Define meaningful benefit/harm thresholds and analysis choices before examining
future confirmatory results, using justified assay performance and pilot evidence
when available. If not yet justified, label them pending; do not invent cutoffs.
Unexpected findings may motivate exploratory revisions, clearly distinguished
from the original test.

## Completion and continuity

Before advancing a claim or issuing a new scientific snapshot, give each applicable
coverage dimension a documented search/adjudication or an explicit unresolved gap.
A high-impact unresolved gap blocks the corresponding inference, not unrelated
useful research. End a bounded cycle with reasons for its stopping point (coverage,
diminishing new decision-relevant evidence, access or resource limit) and specific
remaining work. A search count, model consensus or passing software checks is not
evidence that the biology is correct or the search exhaustive.

Reopen the search when a new candidate, model, endpoint, primary result, adverse
signal, correction, trial result or label change could affect a decision. Recheck
pivotal evidence before any future promotion or release. No automatic background
monitor or new paid model job is established by this objective.

The patient-data restrictions, separate tumour/constitutional assessment, immutable
Track 1 deliverables and all publication/provider gates remain in force. No family
contact, treatment, new biological experiment or portal submission is authorized by
this research-process update.
