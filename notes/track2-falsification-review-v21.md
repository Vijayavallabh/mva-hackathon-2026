# Track 2 v21: falsification changes the research plan

27 September 2026 · Session 61 · Review of v20, the v15 drug decisions and the
completed v19 public-expression campaign. No new biological experiment or model
inference. This is an agent-led audit, not independent scientific peer review.

**No drug earns rescue priority.** Everolimus remains an optional, qualified-model
probe; HCQ remains reserve. The useful change is a stricter, more informative test:
first establish that the model and assay can answer the question, then distinguish
inconclusive evidence from a result that actually rejects the tested benefit.

## What changed

1. **A failed computational filter is not a biological negative.** The 0/14 result
   has unknown sensitivity and specificity. The six-reagent minimum is an operational
   choice, not a biological boundary. Keep favourable HT29 evidence visible, but do
   not relax the rule after seeing it. Calibrate on held-out known-positive and
   negative perturbations before another drug-ranking campaign. A newly calibrated
   analysis would be exploratory on the existing data and need independent confirmation.
2. **The next experiment must observe drug and deficiency together.** Separate drug-only
   and knockdown-only expression profiles cannot establish this response. Correction
   controls distinguish background effects; benefit need not be exclusive to deficient
   cells to be useful. Claims of genotype-specific rescue require an appropriate
   interaction contrast, not significance in one group and nonsignificance in another.
3. **Function and schedule need direct tests.** The 2026 RAPA-EX-01 trial adds human
   counterevidence to a generic rapalog-muscle-benefit argument. Preserve its primary
   nonsignificant estimate, not just its significant sensitivity results. The final
   2025 PoWeR mouse publication is a counterweight to inevitable muscle harm; its
   preprint is not another replication. Neither study settles everolimus in MVA.
   [Trial](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcsm.70274),
   [PoWeR](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.70183).
4. **HCQ cannot be the sole autophagy mechanism control.** Primary experiments report
   chromatin-timing effects outside an intact lysosomal system. Their model, concentration
   and small replication limit transfer, but the result defeats a selective-control
   assumption. Reopening HCQ requires chromosome-function and delayed-injury checks
   alongside independent flux assays. [Study](https://doi.org/10.1080/15384101.2024.2402191).
5. **NAD/SIRT2 is a competing hypothesis, not a newly selected drug.** Genetic SIRT2
   survival, NMN protein abundance and niacin human muscle findings belong to different
   experiments. They cannot be chained into demonstrated niacin rescue of this pair.
   Preserve the favourable observations and the K250/functional counterevidence.
   [K668/NMN](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.201386907),
   [K250](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2014.09.128),
   [niacin](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2020.04.008).
6. **Make the stopping logic executable.** Invalid controls hold inference; insufficient
   precision holds advancement; a valid interval excluding meaningful benefit stops
   that tested claim; injury triggers stop advancement for review. No missing threshold
   is assigned a convenient value. Every current biological gate remains unknown.

## Coverage of the solution

The [27-claim register](track2-falsification-register-v21.json) carries forward all
19 earlier claims and adds eight challenges. Each has a falsifier, stopping rule,
reopening rule and concrete v21 action. Dependencies describe inference, not proof
that the subject's unknown phase prevents engineered-model research.

| Part | Strongest challenge | Resulting requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Attribution and phase | A trans construct does not establish subject phase; correction may have clonal effects. | Preserve cis/single/trans alternatives, multiple independent edits and endogenous measurements. |
| Protein/DNA models | Related models and residue controls do not independently calibrate disease or rescue. | Retain all control failures; no extra structure runs without a discriminating biological question. |
| Mechanism A/B | Increased signaling may compensate; impaired clearance may worsen with more initiation. | Lock one qualified branch and test orthogonal turnover/pathway perturbation and function. |
| RNAi signature | Shared seed, potency, cell state and off-target effects undermine the null. | Use matched controls when metadata permit; unknown metadata blocks calibrated significance. |
| HT29 exception | Five reagents and post-hoc transformation selection weaken confirmation. | Keep the favourable tail and failed full gate together; obtain independent validation. |
| Compound/exposure | Stereochemical metadata and low-activity QC cannot settle vial identity or no effect. | Verify material and engagement; never pool unresolved IDs into a dose curve. |
| Endpoints | Slower division, cell loss and selective clonal expansion can mimic improvement. | All-enrolled output, competing fates, daughter survival, lineage function and recovery. |
| Statistics | Wells, clones, days and donors answer different questions; nonsignificance is not safety. | Randomize at the treatment unit; preserve nesting and crossed blocks; use justified interval bounds. |
| Tumour and normal tissue | Cancer killing can injure deficient normal cells; restoration can support some tumours. | Separate subtype-qualified tumour and deficient-normal outcomes at matched exposure. |
| Alternatives | Rejected drugs, vitamins and binders can inherit unjustified priority. | Same function, exposure and injury standard for all candidates; retain eleven dispositions. |
| Report and harness | Passing code tests can look like validation; repeated records inflate study counts. | Mark software scope, deduplicate sources and preserve unmet biological/delivery gates. |

## Search and source adjudication

Public web discovery used 31 neutral, contrary and alternative-mechanism queries,
followed by primary publisher/PMC passages, an official label and trial records.
The complementary scripted search attempted 16 Europe PMC queries (all HTTP 503)
and 16 PubMed E-utilities queries across initial/fallback runs. Failed access is
not negative evidence. See the [web log](track2-falsification-web-v21.json),
[initial metadata log](track2-falsification-search-v21.json) and
[fallback log](track2-falsification-search-fallback-v21.json).

This is a bounded targeted review. Returned IDs and titles are discovery records,
not studies read; broad queries return only a first-result window. PubMed expanded
two unmatched phrase queries; their counts are not exact-title search counts.
The initial PubMed run did not retain translation/warning fields. A follow-up
metadata fetch deduplicated 249 identifiers; title/notice triage is not 249 full-paper
reviews. The [metadata record](track2-falsification-metadata-v21.json) exposed a
published niacin-methods correction, subsequently read in the author-hosted paper;
the authors state its findings are unchanged.
[Correction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2020.05.020). Twelve selected
adjudication records appear in the [v21 evidence amendment](track2-evidence-v21.json),
including two rechecks of existing sources and a protocol linked to its result.
Reading depth ranges from indexed abstracts to selected methods/results. Direct
ANZCTR retrieval failed; protocol amendment history remains unverified. No complete
trial-registry census, independent extraction, meta-analysis or exhaustive correction
search is claimed. Competitor entries, reviews and model summaries are not evidence.

The prior 63-source ledger remains intact. Broad discovery revisited HCQ,
pralatrexate, temsirolimus, entinostat, vorinostat, niclosamide, posaconazole,
PP2A tools, senolysis and readthrough; no retrieved source supplied the missing
selected-pair joint functional/exposure/safety chain. This is not proof no such
evidence exists. The new NAD comparison also fails that chain and is not promoted.

## Next discriminating work and stopping point

An authorized laboratory must first establish a relevant endogenous deficit and
measurement range, with independent genetic correction/perturbation controls. Then
lock compound, branch, biological stage, randomized unit, function endpoint, injury
bounds and confirmation design. The [v21 validation plan](track2-validation-v21.md)
specifies the order and alternative outcomes.

Another large model or correlation run cannot currently resolve phase, endogenous
function, joint drug response or free-tissue exposure. We stop this literature cycle
after adjudicating the material new contradictions and counterweights. Remaining
uncertainties are named in the register; this is neither an exhaustive search nor
completion of Track 2. Reopen it for a new relevant primary result, correction,
qualified assay, exposure measurement or candidate—not to force a preferred answer.

```bash
uv run --no-project python scripts/track2_falsification_v21.py
uv run python -m unittest discover -s scripts -p 'test_track2_falsification_v21.py'
```

The checker verifies the public amendment and proposed decision logic. Synthetic
regression cases are not experiments. The actual contract returns **HOLD**, with
null benefit/injury margins and no clinical recommendation.
