Reviewed 2026-09-09 against the 53-source ledger and the review specification in
`notes/track2-final-review.md`. This is a bounded public-literature supplement, not a
treatment recommendation, systematic review, or replacement for the immutable Track 2

## Decision

The new evidence strengthens falsification and safety gates; it does not establish a
drug-rescue result or a therapeutic margin. Everolimus remains a conditional pathway
experiment and hydroxychloroquine remains reserve. Pralatrexate is a substantive new
**fusion-positive RMS tumour-only horizon lead**, not a replacement constitutional-rescue
priority. Trans phase remains unconfirmed. No proposed experiment has been performed.

The most important additions are: a genuine rapamycin/slippage hazard under mitotic
stress; a negative SIRT2 experiment in a different ageing context; exclusion of a
rapamycin-triggered laboratory tool from therapeutic evidence; and stronger distinctions
between nominal antiproliferative potency, colony formation, regression and recurrence.

## Verified additions and their limits

### 1. Rapamycin can change the fate of mitotically arrested cells

In the human experiment, A549 lung-cancer cells underwent nocodazole arrest (2 µM), then
received **rapamycin 100 nM**, with 60-hour live imaging. Slippage increased from
33.16% to 46.41% **among cells classified as mitotic death or slippage**, not among all
cells. The human mechanism was not the yeast APC/C-Cdh1 mechanism; mitotic timing did
not shorten, and the authors considered a changed death/slippage balance. This is not an
everolimus experiment, a Torin1 result, a BUB1B-deficient normal-cell test, or demonstrated
clinical harm. [Primary study, Results/Figure 7 and live-imaging Methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2021.103675)

Review inference: a pathway signal and reduced proliferation cannot establish safer
division. Record single-cell mitotic death, slippage, accurate division and subsequent
survival/regrowth separately, including matched deficient-normal and corrected controls.
Test combination context explicitly; nocodazole is not the patient's chemotherapy.
This is a material new counterevidence source, not grounds to assert that every rapalog
increases aneuploidy in every tissue.

### 2. Negative SIRT2 evidence is context-specific, not a failed same-model replication

Wu et al. found no significant lifespan benefit from SIRT2 transgenic overexpression
in ordinary C57BL/6J-background ageing mice: both sexes on chow and males on high-fat
diet. The transgene remained strongly expressed in older animals. These were **not
BubR1-hypomorphic mice**. The paper explicitly distinguishes the earlier hypomorph
benefit; it used the same transgenic strain but a different disease context.
[Wu 2023 primary study](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.14027)

This newly added negative context constrains a general SIRT2/anti-ageing claim. It does
not erase North 2014's model-specific result, test NMN efficacy for the selected pair,
or validate an approved SIRT2 activator. Keep direct BubR1 functional measurements and
drug/formulation eligibility as unresolved gates; do not equate protein overexpression
with administration of a clinically usable compound.

### 3. A rapamycin dimerizer experiment is not rapalog rescue

Allan et al.'s directSLiMs system used engineered HeLa cells, FRB/FKBP components and a
recruited LxxIxE peptide to inhibit PP2A-B56 substrate interactions. Rapamycin was a
**chemical dimerization trigger**. A mutant-FRB-selective A/C heterodimerizer reproduced
the effect without TORC1 inhibition, separating the engineered mechanism from mTOR.
PP2A-B56 inhibition disrupted metaphase chromosome attachment; the peptide-mutant
control did not reproduce it. [Allan 2025 primary study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-58185-8)

This is a new assay/control source, not evidence that rapamycin restores deficient
BubR1 or that clinical everolimus directly inhibits PP2A-B56. A BubR1 phosphosite change
must not be scored as rescue without attachment, alignment and accurate-segregation
outcomes. The Aurora-B inhibitor used to reverse an engineered attachment phenotype
also does not become an approved MVA drug candidate by association.

### 4. Pralatrexate: stronger new tumour evidence, with recurrence and schedule toxicity

The 2026 fusion-positive RMS study identified TYMS dependence and tested antifolates.
Pralatrexate inhibited several human FP-RMS lines and engineered models covering seven
oncofusions; the 96-hour RH4 CellTiter-Glo IC50 was 0.3712 nM. In mice it produced
complete RH4 regressions followed by recurrence after withdrawal, suppressed RH28/RH30
growth, and inhibited one PDX model with some regressions. Thus the result is not merely
cytostasis, but neither is it durable eradication. Concurrent pralatrexate/vinorelbine
was toxic; an alternating schedule regressed all seven reported RH28 tumours, with
later regrowth. Weekly vinorelbine toxicity limited analysis after three doses; the
remaining active schedules stopped at week seven because of weight loss.
[Primary study, Results/Figures 9–10 and treatment Methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73749-y)

Pralatrexate's US label is for relapsed/refractory peripheral T-cell lymphoma, not RMS.
Pediatric safety/effectiveness are not established; mucositis, myelosuppression and
renal-impairment exposure risks matter. [Official label, indications, warnings and pediatric use](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=cc3df863-c51c-49c5-283a-d6808fb49258&version=6)

Review inference: retain as a subtype-conditioned horizon candidate. Do not assume a
fusion, infer selective safety from mouse weight alone, or equate a nominal medium IC50
with free human tumour exposure. Require oncology-reviewer comparator selection,
exposure scheduling, deficient-normal safety and post-withdrawal durability before
promotion. The source's TYMS dependency does not make pralatrexate a selective TYMS
inhibitor or a chromosome-segregation repair drug.

### 5. Disulfiram, selenite and palbociclib: potency and durable effects disagree

Chico et al. tested one RMS line, RD, alongside two osteosarcoma lines. RD 72-hour SRB
IC50 values were disulfiram **0.19 µM**, sodium selenite **6.03 µM** and palbociclib
**13.25 µM** in 10% serum medium. These are nominal concentrations and a biomass-based
endpoint. Despite the lowest IC50, disulfiram did **not** match the near-complete
post-treatment colony suppression reported for selenite/palbociclib in RD; its comparable
effect was in Saos-2 osteosarcoma. CAM treatment applied 40 µL at five times IC50 locally,
not a measured human systemic exposure. [Chico 2026, Methods and Results/Tables 1 and Figures 1–5](https://doi.org/10.1002/ddr.70304)

Review inference: do not rank by SRB potency alone or translate a short CAM experiment
into RMS maintenance efficacy. LC3-II and LysoTracker changes do not by themselves
establish flux direction or autophagy-dependent death. No matched BUB1B-deficient normal
window is supplied. These remain weak, exposure-unbridged tumour-only horizons; this
paper does not justify HCQ promotion or treating CDK4/6-associated arrest as rescue.

### 6. Statin sensitization is a tumour-damage proposal, not muscle rescue

Codenotti et al. studied RD/RH30 and engineered radioresistant RMS derivatives. Nominal
lovastatin 10 µM or simvastatin 5 µM, with two-hour pretreatment before 4 Gy irradiation,
reduced clonogenic survival; apoptosis and persistent DNA-damage signals supported
sensitization. Culture used 10% serum. An actinomycin-D combination was also examined.
[Codenotti 2024 primary study, selected Methods and Results 3.3](https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers16050853)

This newly identified cell-culture evidence is stronger than a pathway-only association,
but supplies no verified free-exposure bridge or matched deficient-normal selectivity.
For a constitutional chromosome-instability proposal, tumour oxidative injury is not
normal-muscle rescue. Any follow-up requires normal-muscle injury, accurate-division,
combination and exposure gates; clinical lipid-lowering experience cannot substitute
for testing the oncology concentrations and schedule.

## Search gaps and already-known evidence

The original Firecrawl results rediscover Sieben's allele/model paper, its correction,
North's hypomorph work, Tang's aneuploid antiproliferation study, rapalog trial literature
and the posaconazole horizon already mentioned in the final review. Rediscovery is not
independent replication. Correction notices, reviews and primary reports are not
interchangeable. Preserve the existing clinical response exceptions and negative trials;
this supplement supplies no newly verified controlled HCQ benefit or selected-pair
functional rescue.

A further new hit, the TBX2/TBX3 drug-repurposing screen, reports niclosamide, piroctone
olamine and pyrvinium pamoate effects in dependent cancer models. Only its primary
abstract was independently read here; compound identity, model-specific endpoints,
systemic exposure and regulatory eligibility have not been sufficiently assessed to
promote it. [Primary abstract, DOI 10.1002/cam4.70303](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39403898/)

Mebendazole search hits included a conference abstract and patent; the focused
posaconazole search also retrieved unrelated settings. Those are discovery leads, not
validated RMS evidence or proof of absence. Forward citations also included additional
clinical/comparator papers that were not fully reviewed in this bounded supplement.
They remain a coverage gap, not an assertion that the 53-source baseline contained every
relevant trial.

## Integration requirements

Retain the existing phenotype/allele, phase, experimental-exposure and matched-normal
gates. Add explicit per-cell slippage and post-slippage fate; distinguish reversible
arrest, sustained normal-cell injury, tumour regression and post-withdrawal regrowth.
Keep molecular target engagement subordinate to functional outcomes. Any new v3
research report should preserve v2, cite both favourable and limiting observations,
label all horizons and disclose newly used services without implying that discovery
tools established efficacy. No entry upload, drug administration or altered submitted
Track 1 deliverable is authorized by this review.
