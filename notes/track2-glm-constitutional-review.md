Independent primary-source review, 2026-09-09, feat-009. Scope: constitutional
BUBR1 function, PP2A-B56 recruitment/stabilization, protein homeostasis,
readthrough, and senescence. The comparator is the existing 53-source ledger plus

## Conclusion

The strongest addition is a **mechanistic rescue-control opportunity**, not a new
approved-drug lead: engineered kinetochore recruitment of PP2A-B56 improved
chromosome alignment in two published MVA-derived cell lines. That endpoint was
measured during an experimentally imposed metaphase arrest and is not proof of
normal daughter-cell genomes. The review also adds substantive counterevidence:
disputed PP2A-compound specificity, opposing tissue effects of senescence
perturbation, and a conflict between HSP90-based senolysis and mutant-BUBR1
stability. No source establishes selected-pair rescue, trans phase, pediatric
clinical efficacy, or an unbound exposure margin. No drug-ranking promotion is
justified by these papers alone.

## Consequential distinctions for the GLM synthesis

### 1. Local recruitment is not global phosphatase activation

C1 transiently expressed a MIS12-linked phosphomimetic KARD construct to recruit
PP2A-B56 to outer kinetochores. It improved alignment in both published MVA cell
lines; a BUBR1 construct unable to bind B56 did not. Cells were exposed to MG132
for three hours before fixed-cell assessment. Separately, ZM447439 at nominal
2 µM for one hour improved alignment in engineered HeLa perturbations. The
chemical experiment must not be merged with the MVA-cell genetic-localization
experiment into a claim of drug rescue in MVA. [C1](https://doi.org/10.1242/bio.20134051)

Review inference: an engineered localization control could test whether a
phenotype is recruitment-responsive. It does not demonstrate that any PP2A
activator reproduces the spatially controlled effect. Alignment and cold-stable
attachments must be complemented by live anaphase accuracy, daughter-cell
viability, subsequent micronuclei/aneuploidy, and proliferation after washout.
Suppressing Aurora-B-dependent error correction can make an attachment stable
without proving that it is correct. This is an assay-design caution, not a new
drug recommendation.

### 2. The PP2A chemical mechanism remains disputed

C2 attributes DT-061 activity to stabilization of assembled B56α-PP2A. C3 used
biochemical assays and chemogenetic profiling and could not reproduce the
proposed PP2A-B56 mechanism; it linked cellular effects to other structures.
C3 explicitly does not exclude a dependence on particular experimental
conditions. Consequently, “PP2A activator” is a reported/disputed annotation for
these compounds, not sufficient causal evidence.
[C2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.03.038),
[C3](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.2022110611)

C4 is genuinely recent supporting biophysics for a **different** molecule:
ATUX-8385 changes PR65 unfolding/refolding, while the nonfunctional comparator
has different effects. The article alternates comparator spellings DBK-766 and
DBK-776; do not silently convert that inconsistency into an established second
compound identity. DT-061 docking in that paper is not a cellular rescue or a
replication of the full disputed pharmacology. Binding-pose claims remain partly
computational. [C4](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacsau.6c00003)

Review inference: any PP2A chemical lead needs compound identity, direct target
engagement, relevant B56 isoform/localization, loss-of-effect genetic controls,
and independent tests of microtubules, ER/Golgi and cell toxicity. These papers
do not establish current approved-drug eligibility, a pediatric formulation, or
a clinically reachable safe exposure for ATUX-8385, DT-061 or iHAP1. Keep them
as research tools/hypotheses pending those gates.

### 3. Lower senescence markers can mask the wrong biological outcome

C5 provides direct BubR1-hypomorph counterevidence to treating p21 reduction as
uniformly beneficial: p53/p21 protects muscle and adipose progenitor function,
whereas the lens response differs. C6 supports D+Q senolysis and functional
outcomes in other models, but does not turn an Ercc1 intervention into BubR1
rescue. C7 adds a distinct genetic model in which removal of p16-high cells
damaged vascular/tissue integrity. These models neither prove all senolytics
harmful nor establish constitutional benefit here.
[C5](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2013.03.028),
[C6](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12344),
[C7](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2020.05.002)

C8 explicitly distinguishes reduced senescence-marker fraction caused by cell
killing, altered phenotype, or proliferation of other cells. Its 17-DMAG
intervention improved a composite healthspan score in Ercc1-deficient mice,
with a kidney marker change but no significant corresponding liver change.
Ganetespib acted in senescent endothelial cells but not the tested preadipocytes.
These are useful positive and limiting findings. They do not show chromosome
repair or universal senolytic selectivity. [C8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-00314-z)

The pre-existing BUBR1 stability paper supplies a specific opposing mechanism:
HSP90 inhibition depleted tested unstable BUBR1 proteins, and proteasome
inhibition blocked that enhanced turnover. Therefore “HSP90 inhibition improves
progeroid phenotypes” and “HSP90 inhibition stabilizes BUBR1” must never be treated
as equivalent. Nor does rescue of protein abundance by MG132 establish restored
division or clinical suitability of proteasome blockade.
[Existing Suijkerbuijk 2010 study](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-09-4319)

Review inference: require absolute viable-cell counts, cell-type-resolved
senescence, apoptosis and regeneration, together with chromosome accuracy and
post-treatment regrowth. Biomarker improvement among survivors is not sufficient.
Approval of one component for another condition does not validate a senolytic
combination, schedule or pediatric safety window.

### 4. A stop codon is not itself a readthrough-response biomarker

C9 provides positive evidence that near-cognate insertion can produce functional
readthrough products in selected systems. C10 found no ataluren rescue in its
tested systems and showed a favorable mouse-context result that disappeared in
the human receptor context. This is genuine context dependence, not proof that
readthrough never works. Require endogenous transcript abundance/NMD assessment,
full-length protein measurement, identity of the inserted residue, and function
using a non-luciferase-only endpoint. No BUB1B-specific readthrough rescue was
identified in this bounded review.
[C9](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1605336113),
[C10](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-01093-9)

Regulatory status must come from current regulators, not older paper introductions.
EMA's page still records the European Commission's 28 March 2025 non-renewal of
Translarna because effectiveness was not confirmed. The positive mechanism study
does not reverse that decision or satisfy the competition's approved-drug gate.
[EMA, rechecked 2026-09-09](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/translarna)

## Provenance and unresolved review limits

Discovery used public gene/mechanism searches, bibliographic DOI/PMID resolution
through Europe PMC, and primary publisher/PMC records. Selected available text
was independently retrieved from the official endpoint pattern
`https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/<PMCID>/fullTextXML` and inspected
with local `uv run python` XML parsing. This parser selected sections; it did not
turn a whole downloaded document into a fully read paper.

Usable XML was obtained for C1, C3, C4, C6, C8 and C10. C2, C5 and C9 XML requests
returned HTTP 404; some direct PMC web requests returned access interstitials.
Those failures were not bypassed and were not treated as negative findings.
Primary abstracts supplied the bounded claims for C2/C5/C7/C9. Figures were read
through captions/text, not independently remeasured; supplementary datasets,
all references, publication corrections and all worldwide regulatory records
were not exhaustively audited. This remains a focused scoping/claim review.

GLM dossiers are a separate, model-generated synthesis layer. Their consequential
claims are adjudicated below when actual saved outputs are available; model
completion and source retrieval do not by themselves validate an assertion.
Existing v3-bound files and snapshots remain unchanged.

### PP2A challenge: mechanistic distinctions retained; important errors persist

Retain the separation between kinetochore recruitment, B56-holoenzyme activity,
and isolated PR65 mechanical stabilization; the lack of completed-division
evidence; and the conclusion that the supplied evidence does not establish an
approved constitutional-rescue drug. The inconsistent DBK analogue identifier
in C4 remains a source-level uncertainty, not a reason to invent a correction.

The following challenge claims are **not adopted**:

- Section 6 still assigns Aurora-B-inhibitor rescue to MVA-derived cells.
  The primary experiment distinction remains HeLa pharmacological perturbation
  versus MVA-cell engineered localization rescue. Repetition in a challenge does
  not repair that erroneous merger. [C1](https://doi.org/10.1242/bio.20134051)
- Sections 2 and 7 say Vit did not re-examine the cryo-EM structure because the
  abstract does not mention it. The retrieved full text does reassess the
  reported density, including a possible flexible PP2A-C-tail explanation.
  This is not a new cryo-EM reconstruction, but it is a structural
  re-examination. Abstract silence cannot negate a full-text analysis.
  [C3](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.2022110611)
- The description of C4 as using compounds other than DT-061 is appropriate for
  its core optical-tweezer experiment, not the entire paper: the full text also
  contains DT-061 docking. Neither the docking nor the different-compound
  biophysics settles the cellular specificity dispute.
  [C4](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacsau.6c00003)
- The proposed ordinary-isotope-labeled DT-061 cryo-EM difference-map strategy
  remains an unvalidated methodological suggestion, for the contrast reasons
  explained above. Nor would one negative binding condition or the temporal
  ordering of toxicity universally resolve all mechanisms. Do not convert this
  model-generated design into an executable or decisive protocol.

### Senescence challenge: central corrections accepted with explicit scope

Retain the corrected direction of p21's lens effect, its opposite protective
role in muscle/fat, the BubR1-versus-Ercc1 distinction, and the separation of
genetic population deletion from drug senolysis. Retain cell-type-selective
positive findings in Zhu and the Ercc1 healthspan benefit in Fuhrmann without
turning either into BubR1 drug rescue. The challenge appropriately retracts the
blanket paywall claim and recognizes that abstract-only input is not proof of
inaccessible full text. [C5](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2013.03.028),
[C6](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12344),
[C8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-00314-z)

Residual limits:

- Its statement that a particular DOI resolver is subscription-gated is still
  not a verified access result. Metadata can identify an access route but does
  not prove a successful or failed retrieval in this run.
- “No senolysis evidence exists in any BubR1-deficient model” is overbroad even
  though the following phrase restricts it to drugs. Use **no BubR1 drug-senolysis
  experiment identified in these four sources**. The existing source ledger
  already includes genetic senescent-cell clearance in BubR1 mice; that must not
  disappear from the broader synthesis.
  [Baker 2011](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10600)
- Abstract absence of toxicity, negative results, or protective-cell controls is
  not study-wide absence. Our full-text checks already identify cell-type
  nonresponse and tissue-specific biomarker findings in C8. The Grosse harm
  result concerns genetic elimination of particular populations, not proof
  that D+Q or 17-DMAG reproduces that harm.
  [C7](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2020.05.002),
  [C8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-00314-z)
- A proposed multi-model animal study is not performed or adopted. Functional
  gain without liver sinusoidal endothelial-cell loss would address selected
  endpoints, not by itself establish a clinical therapeutic window, exclude
  injury to other normal populations, or establish exposure and tumour
  selectivity. This is methodological inference, not new efficacy evidence.

### Readthrough challenge: corrected assay logic; no efficacy promotion

Retain the explicit acknowledgement that Bolze's JAK/STAT3 functional assay and
PTP1B control came from reviewer full-text checks, not the model's own abstract
retrieval. Retain the repaired requirement to couple barcode abundance to a
readthrough-dependent selection/sort, the removal of an arbitrary library-median
threshold for a specific endogenous context, and the corrected BUBR1
scaffold/checkpoint functional endpoints. These fix synthesis and design errors;
they do not produce experimental rescue evidence.
[C10](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-01093-9)

Residual corrections:

- Similarity between the amino-acid insertion spectrum at premature stops and
  endogenous readthrough does **not demonstrate drug-induced readthrough at
  normal termination codons**. That is a separate safety question requiring
  measurement, not an observed harm established by this abstract. Likewise,
  inferring an analytical method from author identity is unnecessary.
  [C9](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1605336113)
- A targeted peptide spanning the premature stop can support traversal at that
  site, but does not alone establish an intact full-length protein. Orthogonal
  size/terminal-coverage evidence and functional assays would be needed. This
  is assay-design reasoning, not a criticism of an uninspected primary method.
- Generic-library-positive/endogenous-negative discordance does not isolate
  failure to NMD, abundance, or protein function: readthrough itself can differ
  in the endogenous transcript context. Measure the relevant RNA and actual
  endogenous stop traversal before assigning the cause.
- A future positive experiment could provide new evidence in the tested model;
  this bounded review cannot certify it would be the first BUB1B-relevant
  evidence ever. Neither older mechanistic paper establishes current regulatory
  status. The existing official EMA nonrenewal check remains relevant; no
  ataluren promotion follows from the corrected challenge.
  [EMA Translarna](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/translarna)

### Bounded review decision

This independent note now adjudicates five completed review outputs and three
completed constitutional challenge outputs. No further model rerun is needed to
force agreement. Preserve the generated proposals as provenance, with the
retained/corrected/rejected distinctions here controlling scientific reuse.
The useful additions are assay controls, mechanistic distinctions and negative
evidence—not a new established drug, clinical exposure margin, experiment,
selected-pair rescue, or phase confirmation. The existing conditional research
prioritization is not changed by this bounded constitutional review.
