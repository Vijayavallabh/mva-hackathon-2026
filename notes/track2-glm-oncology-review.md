2026-09-09, session 38, feat-009. Public-primary-source research only. This note does

## Decision and scope

Ten primary-paper seeds were supplied to the main agent for substantive multi-paper
GLM dossiers. The useful result is not ten new therapies: it is better separation of
endogenous-target validation, transient activity, durable killing, exposure and safety.
TBX2/TBX3 is a reading-depth upgrade from the prior abstract-only entry; posaconazole
deepens an already-mentioned horizon. Do not count either as a wholly novel discovery.

Entinostat deserves jurisdiction-aware consideration: an official Chinese approval
exists, so a blanket exclusion as universally unapproved would be wrong. This does not
establish pediatric RMS benefit, clinical access, a matched exposure window or superiority
to the existing priorities. Everolimus remains conditional, HCQ reserve; no source here
establishes constitutional BUB1B rescue or resolves trans phase.

## 1. TBX2/TBX3: upgrade the abstract, retain failed validation

The screen used inducible melanoma cells, then endogenous-protein and RMS validation.
RD/RH30 72-hour MTT assays used 10% serum. Reported nominal EC50 values were:

| Compound | RD, µM | RH30, µM |
|---|---:|---:|
| Niclosamide | 6.79 | 11.06 |
| Pyrvinium pamoate | 3.30 | 7.10 |
| Piroctone olamine | Not estimable | 31.29 |

Niclosamide/pyrvinium were tested at 2–10 µM and piroctone at 4–20 µM: the RH30
niclosamide/piroctone estimates exceed the tested maxima. Tacrolimus/vardenafil hits
failed endogenous-target validation; tacrolimus interfered with Tet-On. Pyrvinium's
initial validation required concentration reduction because of extensive killing.
[Primary study, Methods 2.1/2.7 and Results 3.3–3.5](https://doi.org/10.1002/cam4.70303)

Interpretation: this is not nanomolar RMS activity or proof of direct TBX binding.
Knockdown modulation supports context dependence, but altered baseline growth can
complicate a metabolic viability assay. PARP cleavage/senescence markers do not establish
durable tumour clearance. Current jurisdiction, product and route must be verified
before translating historical anthelmintic or shampoo use into an approved systemic
oncology candidate. No deficient-normal selectivity was established.

## 2. Niclosamide exposure: preserve both formulation studies

The original five-participant enzalutamide combination study reported first-dose total
plasma peaks of 35.7–182 ng/mL and dose-limiting gastrointestinal toxicity at its higher
cohort. It closed for futility because tolerated exposure was below its preclinical
target. Day-15 samples lacked dose-relative timing and were excluded from PK analysis.
This is an adult prostate-cancer/formulation result, not a universal impossibility
theorem for niclosamide. [Schweizer 2018, PK Methods and Results](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198389)

A later nine-participant study of **reformulated PDMX1001** with abiraterone/prednisone
reported no first-cycle dose-limiting toxicity at the studied levels, five PSA responses
among eight evaluable participants (nine enrolled),
and two complete PSA responses with radiographic improvement. Without a backbone-only
control these cannot be assigned to niclosamide. Only three patients supplied the
reported exposure samples: total plasma predose 0.31–0.65 µM and one-hour postdose
0.21–0.72 µM. The latter are scheduled samples labelled “peak,” not a serially established
Cmax. Its claimed preclinical target was prostate-specific, not the RMS MTT threshold.
[Parikh 2021, Methods and Results](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-85969-x)

Review inference: preserve the favourable formulation exception without claiming a
pediatric RMS exposure bridge. Neither study supplies free tumour exposure, matched
free-medium activity, deficient-normal injury thresholds or a clinical margin. A new
formulation is not automatically interchangeable with an existing approved product.

## 3. Posaconazole: real growth inhibition, overstated safety rationale

Sun et al. tested RD, RMS-YM and KYM-1; 24-hour pathway assays used 10/25 µM nominal
posaconazole in 10% serum. The 24-hour XTT IC50 was 36.51 µM in RD and 62.62 µM in
RMS-YM. Oral 70 mg/kg daily for two weeks reduced RD xenograft growth/terminal weight
in six mice per arm. This was not demonstrated eradication or a withdrawal-durability
experiment. Liver histology showed damage; mean GPT was higher, but reported GOT/GPT
group differences were not statistically significant. [Sun 2021, selected Methods/Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8493378/)

Reduced SMO/GLI1 expression does not exclude off-target activity; static LC3/TEM findings
do not alone prove increased flux or autophagy-dependent death. Adjacent human tissue
comparisons concerned SMO expression, not drug-exposed normal-cell safety. These are
important limits on the paper's interpretation, not reasons to discard its growth result.

The official label directly undermines a broad “few interactions/long-term safe” claim:
it warns about serious vinca-alkaloid toxicity and contraindicates concomitant sirolimus
and selected CYP3A4-metabolized statins. Its formulations are not automatically
substitutable. [Posaconazole US label, contraindications and interactions](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0bdf77ae-3639-49c1-b7c7-533f9d073084)

No patient regimen is assumed. An oncology/pharmacology review must precede any
experimental combination; do not assemble posaconazole, a statin and a rapalog simply
because each appears separately in a repurposing paper.

## 4. Hedgehog clinical evidence: response belongs to the correct tumour

The sonidegib study enrolled 60 pediatric patients, including four with RMS, plus
16 adults with medulloblastoma. The five reported complete/partial responses were
Hedgehog-activated **medulloblastomas**, not RMS responses. One RMS participant had
reversible grade-4 CPK elevation; growth-plate abnormalities occurred in three children.
The phase-II component closed prematurely. [Kieran 2017, abstract and selected indexed Table 1/Safety text](https://doi.org/10.1093/neuonc/nox109)

These observations oppose assigning SMO-inhibitor efficacy to every GLI1-high tumour
or describing developmental-pathway inhibition as intrinsically safe in a growth-impaired
setting. Sonidegib is not posaconazole: its effects cannot establish the magnitude of
posaconazole risk or efficacy. Preserve pathway-position, tumour-subtype, compound and
developmental-safety gates separately.

## 5. Vorinostat: retain a clinical exception and negative translational evidence

The pediatric phase-I study found PBMC histone acetylation and variable PK, but no
single-agent objective responses; the complete response with retinoid combination
was in **neuroblastoma**, not RMS. The source's 64 enrolled patients are a multipart,
mixed-diagnosis population, not 64 RMS participants. [Fouladi 2010, abstract and indexed response/PK sections](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2009.25.9119)

The PPTP study reported cell-growth inhibition generally at concentrations not
sustainable clinically and no objective xenograft responses. Growth delay is not
regression; a significant EFS comparison alone did not meet its stronger activity
criteria. [Keshelava 2009, abstract and indexed in-vivo Results](https://doi.org/10.1002/pbc.21988)

There is nevertheless a relevant anaplastic-ERMS case: transient shrinkage/stabilization
and symptomatic improvement occurred, followed by progression; dose escalation caused
severe thrombocytopenia/hemorrhage. Its paired randomized PDX experiment, ten mice per
arm, induced necrosis **without inhibiting tumour growth or Ki67**. Do not turn a
clinical exception into a controlled efficacy estimate, or necrotic regions into a
successful PDX response. [Case and PDX primary, Clinical Course and xenograft Results](https://doi.org/10.3389/fonc.2017.00327)

Vorinostat's US indication concerns specified cutaneous T-cell lymphoma; pediatric
safety/effectiveness are not established in its label. The label includes myelosuppression
and gastrointestinal/metabolic risks. [ZOLINZA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=cd86ee78-2781-468b-930c-3c4677bcc092)

Review inference: preserve the case's favourable signal and harms, but do not promote
unselected HDAC inhibition as constitutional rescue or assume all HDAC drugs share the
same tumour activity.

## 6. Entinostat: jurisdiction, schedule and multiplicity change the reading

The 2019 study found modest single-agent activity in Rh10 and mostly no potentiation
of cytotoxic therapy despite histone target engagement. Indexed primary Results add
a small statistically significant Rh36 growth delay: “one active model” must not
be rewritten as “only one statistically significant comparison.” Neither model
regressed on monotherapy. The abstract and indexed Results also report
**Rh18/vincristine antagonism**. This defeats a blanket synergy claim, not every
possible model/schedule. [Kurmasheva 2019, abstract and indexed Results/Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1002/pbc.27820)

The 2024 study reports favourable model-dependent results, but key comparisons are
explicitly unadjusted: CTG-1008 combinations with vinorelbine/cyclophosphamide had
p=0.016/0.028, while CTG-2127 monotherapy had p=0.053. Its mouse **daily** regimen
had total Cmax 4710 nM and AUC0–24 2510 nM·h, compared with a cited human **weekly**
regimen's Cmax 140.8 nM and AUC 3087 nM·h. The authors acknowledge different peaks,
half-lives and schedules. [Chauhan 2024, Results and selected Discussion](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-66545-5)

Review inference: matching one dose's AUC to a differently repeated regimen does not
establish an equivalent concentration-time profile. Peaks differ approximately
33-fold on the reported total scale; neither this arithmetic nor mixing unbound mouse
with total human values creates a therapeutic margin. Preserve the favourable results
without claiming multiplicity-robust universal synergy or using one group's critique
as independent proof that the other study is invalid.

**Regulatory correction:** the NMPA announced Chinese marketing approval in April 2024
for entinostat with an aromatase inhibitor in previously endocrine-treated HR-positive,
HER2-negative advanced/metastatic breast cancer. A preliminary shorthand in this review's
seed messages called it unapproved; that global claim was wrong and was promptly
corrected. No FDA approval or pediatric RMS indication is established by this notice.
[Official NMPA approval](https://english.nmpa.gov.cn/2024-04/30/c_1049690.htm)

Consequently, jurisdiction-aware eligibility may admit an entinostat tumour-only
horizon; it cannot be rejected merely as universally investigational. Compound-specific
exposure, normal-cell safety, documented subtype and comparator requirements still
prevent promotion to a validated intervention. Responses to mocetinostat are not
entinostat responses, even if a shared protein marker changes.

## 7. Two parent-selected follow-ups, separate from the ten seeds

The earlier muscle-lineage study supports **TBX2**, not interchangeable TBX2/TBX3,
repression of PTEN. It used differentiation models, RMS knockdown/dominant-negative
experiments and promoter assays; TBX2 also regulated normal muscle. TBX3 did not
repress PTEN or the tested differentiation genes in C2C12 cells and was undetectable
in that study's RD/RH30 cultures. This is a context/provenance discrepancy to resolve
against later TBX3 claims, not independent proof that either study is invalid. It
does not show drug-mediated rescue of deficient normal muscle.
[TBX2/PTEN primary, selected Results and Cell Culture](https://doi.org/10.1038/onc.2015.486)

The arsenic trioxide (ATO)/itraconazole study adds a stronger endpoint than MTS:
72-hour treatment followed by ten days in standard medium reduced colony formation.
ATO's 96-hour nominal RMS MTS IC50 values were 1.31–3.67 µM, while adult donor
skeletal-muscle cells remained above 50% at 10 µM. All used 10% serum. Itraconazole
alone had IC50 >20 µM, although 5 µM affected colonies and combinations. Responses
were heterogeneous: RH30 spheroids resisted ATO alone, and 5 µM ATO produced 9%
membrane-compromised ZF cells versus 6% in the normal comparator. Selected models
showed additional combination effects; this is not formal universal synergy.
[ATO/itraconazole primary, Methods and Results](https://doi.org/10.3892/ijo.2015.3293)

The normal comparator is useful but not isogenic BUB1B-deficient tissue; favourable
in-vitro separation is not a clinical therapeutic margin. GLI expression and SMO
inhibitor response are not interchangeable. ATO's broader actions and heterogeneous
GLI/protein-death results also prevent assigning every effect solely to Hedgehog.
