# Questions the submission must answer without live Q&A, version 42

**What is the drug proposal?** Test everolimus, an approved mTORC1 inhibitor, as an
optional mechanistic probe in a functionally qualified non-cancer BUB1B model.
No drug has earned rescue priority. HCQ is reserve. No dose or treatment is proposed.

**What connects these variants to the drug?** Four distinct links: the established
gene-level checkpoint/attachment mechanism; unknown endogenous effects of the retained
pair; a downstream mTOR hypothesis from a different mouse context; and established
drug pharmacology without MVA efficacy. The experiment is designed to test the
missing links. First qualify endogenous deficit and independent functional restoration in proposed
engineered hTERT-RPE1; availability remains unconfirmed. The mTOR rationale comes
from heterozygous BubR1+/X753 skeletal muscle and sarcopenia. Excess mTOR activity
is a later branch prerequisite; a flux alternative needs its own locked plan.

**Why everolimus if several results are unfavorable?** Those results remove claims
of validated rescue and require direct functional measurements. They do not establish
universal inefficacy. Everolimus is a defined, approved perturbation for a conditional
mechanistic test. It advances only if model qualification supports that test. All
eleven candidate dispositions and favorable counterweights remain in the report.

**What changed because of falsification?** Functional protein controls prevent
interpreting model scores as residual function. Separate drug/deficit profiles no
longer support rescue. Muscle counterevidence requires force and recovery, not mass
alone. Early-versus-prolonged sensitivity requires daughter fate and sustained
output. Imaging retains a favorable BUB1B lead but rejects a claimed neural gain.

**Did the GPUs improve the drug hypothesis?** They identified where model-based
claims fail and one bounded imaging assay lead. They did not establish drug response.
Full mean optical profiles outperform learned interphase profiles; equal-cap learning
has a favorable exploratory result. Both required practical-gain tests fail. Those
findings are reported together, without selecting only favorable seeds or baselines.

**Do the new readouts make the experiment stronger or impractical?** Current BUBR1
corona/MCC/APC/C studies support measuring checkpoint architecture, and recent
chromosomal-instability work supports mitochondrial and proteostasis injury markers.
They do not establish a drug response. The open question is whether a feasible first
experiment can include these readouts without losing culture-level replication and
all-enrolled daughter fate.

**What is innovative?** An inspectable path from mechanism to a testable drug
experiment, with all-enrolled benefit/injury outcomes, explicit falsifiers and a
small offline decision demo. The individual methods are established. The claim is
the implemented integration and its accessibility, not invention of causal inference
or proof of superior clinical decisions. The teaching-set comparison is synthetic.

**What could help this child or others if independently validated?** A calibrated
allele-to-function assay could strengthen mechanistic characterization. A valid
negative result could stop an unsupported drug route; a positive result could justify
further preclinical work. Neither is a present diagnostic or treatment benefit.

**How is the design patient-oriented?** It prioritizes useful function, preserves
death/arrest/missing outcomes, stops for injury, separates tumour killing from
deficient-normal benefit, and starts with engineered models without requesting a
new family sample. No family consultation, co-design or endorsement is claimed.

**Can another team use it?** Yes, the offline teaching kit and Python replay require
no subject data or GPUs. That proves a portable implementation, not biological
transfer. New tissues, alleles and diseases require fresh qualification and margins.
No external adoption or usability measurement has yet been performed.

**What would falsify the main route?** A valid model/branch qualification that rejects
the prerequisite stops that branch. Valid confirmation excluding meaningful benefit
stops the tested claim. Unacceptable injury stops advancement despite benefit.
Failed assays and wide intervals hold inference or advancement rather than proving
normality or universal drug failure. All-pass permits preclinical review only.

**What is the next investment?** Endogenous mechanism and one qualified non-cancer
functional context, followed by the randomized joint-treatment experiment if eligible.
No laboratory budget, staffing commitment, sample-size estimate or clinical timeline
is justified by the computational results.

**What remains unresolved?** Phase, endogenous function, drug response, clinical
exposure margins, biological qualification and independent confirmation. The required
video, live delivery checks, receipt and provider/distribution questions also remain.
These are different gaps; a software check or prize objective cannot resolve them.
