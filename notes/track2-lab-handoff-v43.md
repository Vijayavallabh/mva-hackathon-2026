# The smallest experiment that can change the research decision, version 43

This is a proposed preclinical sequence, not an executed protocol, a treatment plan
or a laboratory commitment. Everolimus is an optional mechanistic probe; HCQ is reserve.
No rescue-priority drug, selected-pair efficacy, clinical dose or exposure margin is
supported. Resources, culture success, variance and laboratory availability are unknown.

## Primary question and proposed model

Can an engineered endogenous BUB1B deficit be reproduced and functionally improved
by independent, expression-matched BUBR1 correction/restoration? Start with proposed
hTERT-RPE1 as a nonmalignant, editable qualification comparator. It is immortalized,
its retained expression specificity is weak, and neither an available cell line
nor a successful clone or functional assay is claimed. It is not a muscle/renal
surrogate. No biological work is promised before the submission deadline.

Distinguish residual abundance from defective function using a panel: endogenous
RNA/protein, checkpoint maintenance, unchallenged division and chromosome errors,
then daughter fate and useful output/recovery. Mitotic index combines entry, arrest,
escape and death; localization alone does not prove an assembled functional complex.
SIRT2 overexpression is a separate perturbation with other substrates, not a clean
BUBR1-specific restoration. Site assays need validated reagents; availability is unknown.

After functional qualification, test the prerequisite for branch A: excess
mTOR-associated activity with impaired useful function. The mouse rationale is
heterozygous BubR1+/X753 skeletal muscle with sarcopenia relevance, not demonstrated
compound-pair rescue. Everolimus pharmacology supplies a perturbation, not a causal
bridge. Branch B, impaired dynamic flux/regeneration, needs its own justified locked
plan and cannot replace a failed branch after seeing results. Unknown prerequisites
hold advancement; a precise valid negative can close the tested branch in that model.

Branches A and B above are mTOR-related hypotheses. NAD/SIRT2 is a separate
competing residual-protein hypothesis. Retain the male-biased genetic
survival finding separately from precursor abundance evidence. Mouse K243/human
K250 are corresponding residues; favorable K243R oocyte results coexist with harmful
somatic mouse results. No generic K243R positive control or drug-priority change is
justified. See the current [comparison](https://github.com/Vijayavallabh/mva-hackathon-2026/blob/main/notes/track2-candidate-comparison-v42.md), prior [adjudication](https://github.com/Vijayavallabh/mva-hackathon-2026/blob/main/notes/track2-rehearsal-adjudication-v39.md), [public literature addendum](https://github.com/Vijayavallabh/mva-hackathon-2026/blob/main/notes/track2-literature-v41.md) and [official refresh](https://github.com/Vijayavallabh/mva-hackathon-2026/blob/main/notes/track2-requirements-review-v42.md).

## Sequence and stopping decisions

| Stage | Minimum interpretable comparison | Information gained and next action |
|---|---|---|
| Endogenous mechanism | Wild type/mock, each single allele, cis/trans engineered alternatives and corrected derivatives; RNA/protein and attachment/checkpoint measurements. Independent restoration must avoid the same editing/seed artifact. | Distinguish loss of abundance from residual function, failed scaffolding and editing stress. If controls fail, repair the assay. Engineered phase never establishes subject phase. A well-characterized negative result can reject a specific mechanistic claim. |
| Functional qualification | The proposed engineered hTERT-RPE1 comparator with reproducible deficit, direct mTORC1 engagement (p70 S6 kinase/4EBP1 phosphorylation), mitotic lagging-DNA/anaphase-bridge counts, other mTOR-associated readouts and a responsive functional control. Separate independent cultures/guide designs from nested cells. | Decide whether branch A is testable. Elevated signaling without impaired function, impaired function without the branch prerequisite, or branch engagement without useful output/recovery cannot qualify it. A model can fail without proving the child has normal function. |
| Joint intervention | Deficit + vehicle, deficit + everolimus, corrected + vehicle, corrected + everolimus; compound identity, direct mTORC1 engagement, mitotic lagging-DNA/anaphase-bridge counts, blinded scoring and randomized culture blocks. | Estimate benefit in deficient cells and context-specific injury. Orthogonal pathway disagreement limits mechanism attribution. Drug-only expression reversal supplies no substitute. |
| Independent confirmation | Lock meaningful benefit, injury margins, follow-up, missingness, multiplicity and sample size from pilot performance before fresh confirmation. Include independent cultures/backgrounds and matched exposure measurements. | A valid interval excluding the target stops the tested benefit claim. Imprecision holds it. Injury stops advancement. All-pass permits preclinical review only. |

Before the experiment, lock the primary endpoint: useful cellular output per founder cell enrolled before exposure, followed through a prespecified daughter-fate/recovery window. Define direct mTORC1 engagement as reduced p70 S6 kinase and 4EBP1 phosphorylation in the same context. Independent units are cultures nested within clone/day blocks; cells are nested observations, not independent replicates. A result is meaningful only after pilot variance fixes a justified margin; injury overrides apparent benefit.

Start with the model qualification before commissioning organ-specific work. For a
qualified muscle context, a candidate functional endpoint is contraction/force with
recovery after the defined challenge; mass or ATP alone is insufficient. For a
qualified renal context, select and validate a lineage-appropriate transport or
barrier function. These are proposed assay families, not established surrogate
endpoints for this child. A dividing precursor and a mature non-dividing cell need
different functional endpoints. No numerical thresholds or costs are justified yet.

Enroll cells before exposure. Keep accurate/erroneous first divisions, death, persistent arrest, slippage and missing tracks separately. Measure later daughter
fate and sustained output over a window justified by the model's untreated division
and recovery. Report all enrolled units and biological culture-level uncertainty.
The [demo](https://github.com/Vijayavallabh/mva-hackathon-2026/blob/main/notes/track2-demo-v37.html) explains denominator and missingness traps with
invented examples; its values cannot set real assay thresholds.

## What could matter for people with MVA

A validated allele-to-function assay could improve mechanistic characterization of
similar variants. That would need pathogenic/retained-function controls, calibration
and independent validation before any diagnostic claim. A valid negative result can
prevent spending scarce experimental material on an unsupported drug route. A positive
four-arm drug result could justify the next preclinical study. None establishes
benefit for a person, and different tissues/genotypes need their own qualification.

The first proposed work uses engineered models and existing public evidence. It does
not require a new sample or contact with the family. Any later patient-derived study
would need appropriate authorization and clinical/ethical oversight; no access is
assumed. Prioritizing useful function, avoiding survivor-only ratios and stopping for
injury are concrete patient-oriented choices. They were not co-designed with this
family; no preferences, endorsement or consultation are invented.

## Resources and handoff

Needed capabilities are endogenous model engineering, quantitative RNA/protein
measurement, live-cell fate imaging, a qualified functional assay, compound/exposure
measurement and statistical design. Each lab should cost its own stage after a
feasibility review. Neither a calendar promise nor a sample-size number can be derived
from the computational data. The public schema and stop rules transfer; biological
qualification, injury limits and exposure assumptions do not.

The new 2026 antimitotic BUBR1/Mad1/BubR1/TPR and BUB1B-docking studies reinforce this
boundary. They report tumour-cell killing or cancer-model docking, not useful function
in a deficient-normal model. Griseofulvin, norlichexanthone and vorinostat remain
excluded from the constitutional-MVA branch. The public schema and stop rules still
transfer; tumour-killing endpoints do not.
