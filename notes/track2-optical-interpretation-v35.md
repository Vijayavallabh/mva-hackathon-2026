# What could make the optical result misleading?

19 October 2026. Focused source-method reread, not a systematic review. Two bounded
web searches and their selected primary sources are recorded in the local manifest
under `results/feat009/optical-v35/interpretation-followup/`. Only public-literature
requests and local document parsing were added in this follow-up.

**Aggregation matters.** Funk et al. use median cell-to-guide and guide-to-gene
summaries. Our original retrieval benchmark used clipped means and mean gene
references. The added full-guide median sensitivity tests this omission, while
retaining the original test cohort and reference policy. It is explicitly
exploratory and does not reproduce the published PHATE analysis.
[Methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.10.017).

**A class label is not a fate measurement.** The fixed-cell classifier used 2,514
annotated cells and 182 selected features; measurements were normalized separately
within mitotic and interphase classes. Our inference: changed morphology could
affect classification as well as reflect altered cell-cycle behavior. BUB1B-specific
misclassification has not been measured. A low mitotic fraction could also reflect
reduced entry, faster exit or cell loss. Preserve these alternatives rather than
label a fraction change as rescue. Original raw-feature normalization and unbiased
longitudinal fate cannot be reconstructed from this normalized fixed-cell export.
[Methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.10.017).

**An early favorable result may reverse.** Cohen-Sharir et al. compare diploid/near-
diploid and aneuploid experimental cells. BUB1B/MAD2/TTK knockdown and TTK inhibitors
show relative-sensitivity reversals between early and prolonged assessment. Their
long experiments include splitting/recovery and renewed perturbation; these are
not continuous, all-enrolled daughter trajectories. This supports the need to
separate immediate phenotype, subsequent survival and sustained output. It does
not establish an everolimus response, the selected alleles' effects or safety in a
child. [Study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-03114-6).

The proposal should therefore retain complete enrollment and tracking-loss counts,
first-division fidelity, daughter fate and useful function through a prespecified
follow-up and recovery period. Set that window using the qualified model's division
rate and untreated recovery; a single favorable snapshot cannot close the gate.
Use competent and deficit-matched non-cancer controls. Keep tumour killing separate.
