# Optical evidence: changes to the next biological test

The study tests practical gene retrieval in public HeLa images. A computational
benchmark cannot qualify non-cancer rescue or the retained alleles. Preserve the
v21 decision contract and all prior validation addenda; none of their unknown
biological measurements or exposure margins becomes a numerical result here.

1. Check source coverage and feature provenance before a model result enters the
   report. The four centroid fields invalidate original-fit gain claims; the empty
   source shard needs typed handling without dropping neighboring observations.
2. Verify distinct guide designs and relevant cells separately for each endpoint.
   BUB1B mitotic retrieval is missing under the fixed rule. Interphase coverage
   supports a comparison but does not establish restoration or useful function.
3. Require a strong simple comparator, including source-motivated aggregation.
   Keep practical full-data performance separate from equal-information sensitivity.
   Label the median and matched-sample analyses exploratory; preserve their timing.
4. Allocate independent perturbations across acquisition batches. The present
   pooled encoder and changing plate reference cohorts cannot establish unseen-batch
   generalization. Collect sufficient coverage before interpreting a missing plate
   result as biological evidence.
5. Treat class fractions as observed sample composition. Validate classification
   and account for entry, exit, loss and timing. Enroll before exposure, retain
   tracking-loss counts, and measure accurate first division, daughter fate and
   sustained lineage function through a locked follow-up and recovery period.
6. Test everolimus jointly with a qualified deficit against each alone and corrected
   controls. Use independently treated cultures, randomized allocation and blinded
   scoring. Independent confirmation must clear justified benefit and injury
   margins; safety failure overrides benefit. Exposure remains an additional gate.

The timing revision follows selected primary methods/results, not a new biological
observation. [Source reread](track2-optical-interpretation-v35.md). The six new
[falsification records](track2-optical-register-v36.json) bring the retained total
to 58 across seven registers. They are claims, not 58 independent studies.
