# V31 qualification amendments

Apply these with the unchanged [v21 biological safeguards](track2-validation-v21.md)
and later v23/v25/v27/v29 amendments. The new data change the qualification requirements,
not the drug ledger. Unknown evidence returns HOLD; failed safety overrides apparent
benefit. All-pass permits only further preclinical review.

| Requirement | V31 observation | Stop or reopening rule |
|---|---|---|
| Demonstrate the perturbation and its attribution separately | BUB1B RNA is suppressed; RPE1 expression is detectable but poorly distinguishes its own held-out cells among essential-gene responses. | Do not qualify the model from RNA suppression or expression magnitude alone. Require independent perturbation/restoration and relevant functional consequences. |
| Test the unselected control population | Retained non-targeting controls exactly equal provider-selected core controls. | Mark the planned sensitivity unavailable. Recover an appropriate independent control population before claiming robust null calibration. |
| Match the relevant cellular context | BUB1B RPE1–K562 matching is weak despite stronger MTOR matching. Cells also differ in time point, CRISPRi effector and growth conditions. | Do not pool or transfer drug rankings across cells. Reopen transfer only with demonstrated common functional biology and relevant tissue response. |
| Preserve completeness and distinguish missing states | RPE1 CDC20/AURKB have 15/5 cells, below the fixed 30-cell retrieval requirement; RICTOR is absent; RPE1 RPTOR transcript is unmeasured. | No imputed zero effect, absent-target negative control or post-hoc lowering of the coverage gate. |
| Separate repeatability from independent confirmation | Two guides occupy one shared construct; all repeated splits reuse the same cells. | No biological confidence intervals or replication count from cell resampling. Restore gene function and use an independent construct before attribution. |
| Verify pathway direction and harmful outcomes | Literature supports context-dependent BUB1B/mTOR roles and additional chromosomal-injury mechanisms under TORC1 inhibition in yeast. | Require measured pathway state plus division, cohesion, damage, daughter fate and later non-cancer function. A transcriptomic reversal does not override injury. |
| Treat clinical evidence by population and comparison | Negative primary tumour endpoints coexist with exploratory response signals and favorable comparative tolerability in selected pediatric settings. | Neither universal toxicity nor universal safety/benefit is established. Keep clinical margins null; do not import another disease's regimen or exposure. |

The data support RPE1 as a potential **functional-assay comparator**, not a qualified
disease surrogate. No drug-plus-BUB1B-deficit response was measured. They do not recreate
the selected alleles, establish phase, resolve tumour versus non-cancer benefit, or
justify treatment. Everolimus remains an optional model-qualified probe; HCQ is reserve.
