## Narration

### Slide 1 / 0:00–0:22 / The question

Can everolimus improve tissue function in mosaic variegated aneuploidy, or MVA?
BUB1B encodes BUBR1, which helps control chromosome attachment and division timing.
Our proposal tests benefit and harm in non-cancer cells. Everolimus remains a
mechanistic probe; no drug has earned rescue priority.

### Slide 2 / 0:22–0:52 / Conflicting evidence

We actively searched for evidence against our approach. Everolimus improved selected
muscle-mass outcomes in aged rats, without establishing force improvement. But
rapamycin impaired regeneration in injured mouse muscle, and everolimus reduced
BUBR1 protein in late-passage human stromal cells. These different models do not
establish benefit or harm for our variants. They require us to test dose, tissue
state and recovery separately.

### Slide 3 / 0:52–1:24 / Separate hypotheses

The review also challenged our entry rule. Rapamycin improved flux and regeneration
in a hypercapnia model without elevated baseline measured mTOR. That is not evidence
of everolimus benefit in MVA. We now separate two hypotheses: branch A requires
excess mTOR activity and a functional deficit; branch B requires impaired autophagic
flux and regeneration. Neither is established here. Prespecify each test; do not
switch hypotheses after failure.

### Slide 4 / 1:24–1:47 / The tumour boundary

Tumour evidence answers a different question. In ARST1431, adding temsirolimus to
chemotherapy did not establish an event-free survival benefit among 297 evaluable
participants. The hazard ratio was zero point eight six; its confidence interval
crossed one. This neither proves zero effect nor tests non-cancer everolimus rescue.

### Slide 5 / 1:47–2:21 / The experiment

Compare deficient and corrected models with vehicle and everolimus. Include phase
controls, randomize treatment and blind scoring. Test graded exposure, regeneration,
recovery and delayed injury. Measure tissue function, BUBR1 and dynamic autophagic
flux. Track every enrolled cell: first-division accuracy, death, arrest and slippage;
record daughter survival and tracking loss separately. Removing abnormal cells must
not masquerade as rescue. Independent pathway controls help test the mechanism.

### Slide 6 / 2:21–3:00 / The decision

Advance only with functional benefit, acceptable safety, justified exposure and
replication. Whole-blood levels do not establish free tissue exposure. HCQ remains
reserve; reported myopathy strengthens delayed muscle-safety checks. Stop advancement
if a criterion fails or remains unresolved. Unexplained BUBR1 loss also pauses advancement.
Phase is unconfirmed and clinical exposure margins remain unknown; no experiments were performed. We disclose OpenAI, Google DeepMind and
Fireworks GLM assistance; Fireworks training and retention remain unverified. We thank
the child, family and organizers.
