## Narration

### Slide 1 / 0:00–0:28 / Candidate and mechanism

Everolimus is an approved mTORC1 inhibitor. We propose testing a new use: downstream
tissue function in BUB1B-associated MVA. Our selected stop-gain suggests loss of
function; the missense effect and phase remain unconfirmed. Everolimus is a
mechanistic probe in qualified non-cancer models, not an established treatment or
correction of the gene.

### Slide 2 / 0:28–0:53 / Conflicting evidence

We searched for evidence against the idea. Everolimus improved selected muscle-mass
outcomes in aged rats without establishing force improvement. Rapamycin impaired
regeneration in injured mouse muscle, while everolimus reduced BUBR1 protein in
late-passage human stromal cells. These different models establish neither benefit
nor harm for our variants.

### Slide 3 / 0:53–1:19 / Separate hypotheses

Another model challenged our entry rule: rapamycin improved flux and regeneration
during hypercapnia without elevated baseline measured mTOR. That does not establish
everolimus benefit here. Branch A requires excess mTOR activity and a functional
deficit; branch B requires impaired autophagic flux and regeneration. Neither is
established. Prespecify each test; no switching after failure.

### Slide 4 / 1:19–1:39 / The tumour boundary

ARST1431 provides a separate boundary. Adding temsirolimus to chemotherapy did not
establish an event-free survival benefit among 297 evaluable participants. The hazard
ratio was zero point eight six, with uncertainty crossing one. This does not test
non-cancer everolimus rescue.

### Slide 5 / 1:39–2:09 / The experiment

Compare deficient and corrected models, with phase controls, randomized treatment
and blinded scoring. Measure function, BUBR1, dynamic flux, regeneration, recovery
and delayed injury across graded exposures. Track first division, death, arrest
and slippage; follow daughter survival and tracking loss separately. Independent
pathway controls test mechanism. Losing abnormal cells must not masquerade as rescue.

### Slide 6 / 2:09–2:46 / Impact, innovation and reuse

If validated, this could clarify what helps or harms MVA cells. Our contribution
combines genotype-aware models with complete cell-fate accounting. Others can reuse
the open code and decision rules, but must requalify each model. Advance only with
function, safety, justified exposure and replication; whole blood is not free tissue
exposure. Stop if criteria fail or remain unresolved; pause unexplained BUBR1 loss.
HCQ remains reserve. No rescue priority, clinical margin or experimental result is established.

### Slide 7 / 2:46–3:00 / Acknowledgement

We disclose OpenAI, Google DeepMind and Fireworks GLM assistance; Fireworks training
and retention remain unverified. We thank the child, family and organizers.
