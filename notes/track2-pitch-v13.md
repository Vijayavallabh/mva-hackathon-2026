## Narration

### Slide 1 / 0:00–0:23 / Candidate and mechanism

Everolimus is an approved mTORC1 inhibitor. We propose testing downstream tissue
function in BUB1B-associated MVA. The stop-gain suggests loss of function; the
missense effect and phase remain unconfirmed. This is a mechanistic probe in
qualified non-cancer models, not an established treatment.

### Slide 2 / 0:23–0:49 / Models challenged our claim

Expanded modeling weakened our earlier claim. Seven protein-sequence checkpoints
disagree and fail selected controls. AlphaFold2 and Boltz produce confident folds
even for impaired controls. Evo2's public BRCA1 benchmark does not validate BUB1B.
We still need endogenous RNA, protein and cell-function measurements.

### Slide 3 / 0:49–1:09 / Conflicting drug evidence

Drug evidence also conflicts. Everolimus improved selected muscle-mass outcomes
without established force improvement. Rapamycin impaired muscle regeneration;
everolimus reduced BUBR1 in late-passage stromal cells. These different models
establish neither benefit nor harm for our variants.

### Slide 4 / 1:09–1:33 / Separate hypotheses

Rapamycin improved flux and regeneration in another model without elevated baseline
measured mTOR. This does not establish everolimus benefit here. Branch A requires
excess mTOR activity and a functional deficit; branch B requires impaired flux and
regeneration. Neither is established. Prespecify separate tests; no switching after failure.

### Slide 5 / 1:33–1:49 / The tumour boundary

In ARST1431, adding temsirolimus to chemotherapy did not establish an event-free
survival benefit. The hazard ratio was zero point eight six, with uncertainty
crossing one. This does not test non-cancer everolimus rescue.

### Slide 6 / 1:49–2:16 / The experiment

Compare deficient and corrected models with phase controls, randomized treatment
and blinded scoring. Measure function, BUBR1, flux, regeneration, recovery and delayed
injury across graded exposures. Track first division, death, arrest and slippage;
follow daughter survival and tracking loss separately. Losing abnormal cells must
not masquerade as rescue.

### Slide 7 / 2:16–2:46 / Impact and decisions

The contribution combines genotype-aware models with complete cell-fate accounting.
Others can reuse the open methods, but must requalify each model. Advance only with
function, safety, justified exposure and replication. Whole blood is not free tissue
exposure. Stop if criteria remain unmet; pause unexplained BUBR1 loss. HCQ stays reserve.
No rescue priority or clinical margin is established. No wet-lab experiment was performed.

We disclose OpenAI, Atlas, Fireworks, owner-hosted biomolecular models and ColabFold
alignment search. Fireworks and alignment-service policies remain unverified.
We thank the child, family and organizers.
