## Narration

### Slide 1 / 0:00–0:21 / Candidate and mechanism

Everolimus is an approved mTORC1 inhibitor. We propose testing downstream tissue function in BUB1B-associated MVA. The stop-gain suggests loss of function; missense effect and phase remain unconfirmed. Everolimus remains a mechanistic probe, not an established treatment.

### Slide 2 / 0:21–0:43 / New sequence evidence

Newer ESMC and ESM3 sequence models pass the small fixed control challenge and score N1002K negatively. That strengthens the reason to test the allele. Earlier models disagree and fail selected controls. These correlated predictions cannot establish allele function or drug response.

### Slide 3 / 0:43–1:05 / Structure and DNA limits

AlphaFold3 and ESMFold2 confidently fold impaired controls. ESM3 structures vary substantially across wild-type seeds. Evo2 twenty-billion passes the reused BRCA1 benchmark, which does not validate BUB1B. Endogenous RNA, protein and cell function still need measurement.

### Slide 4 / 1:05–1:22 / Conflicting drug evidence

Drug evidence conflicts too. Everolimus improved selected muscle-mass outcomes without established force improvement. Rapamycin impaired regeneration; everolimus reduced BUBR1 in late-passage stromal cells. Different models establish neither benefit nor harm for our variants.

### Slide 5 / 1:22–1:43 / Separate hypotheses

Another model showed rapamycin benefit without elevated baseline measured mTOR. That does not establish everolimus benefit here. Branch A requires excess mTOR and a functional deficit; branch B requires impaired flux and regeneration. Neither is established. No switching after failure.

### Slide 6 / 1:43–1:58 / The tumour boundary

In ARST1431, adding temsirolimus did not establish an event-free survival benefit. The hazard ratio was zero point eight six, with uncertainty crossing one. This does not test non-cancer everolimus rescue.

### Slide 7 / 1:58–2:22 / The experiment

Compare deficient and corrected models with phase controls, randomized treatment and blinded scoring. Measure function, BUBR1, flux, regeneration, recovery and delayed injury across graded exposures. Track first division, death, arrest and slippage; daughter survival and tracking loss separately. Cell loss must not masquerade as rescue.

### Slide 8 / 2:22–2:47 / Impact and decisions

Others can reuse the genotype-aware methods and cell-fate accounting, but must requalify each model. Advance only with function, safety, exposure and replication. Whole blood is not free tissue exposure. Stop if criteria remain unmet; pause unexplained BUBR1 loss. HCQ stays reserve. No rescue priority, clinical margin or wet-lab result is established.

We disclose the AI providers, owner-hosted models and alignment service, with unresolved service policies and AlphaFold3 output terms. We thank the child, family and organizers.
