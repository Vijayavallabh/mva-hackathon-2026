### Slide 1 / 0:00–0:22 / The proposed candidate

### Slide 2 / 0:22–0:46 / A conditional mechanism

Our genetic starting point is a stop-gain and missense BUB1B pair. Phase and endogenous effects remain unresolved. Different-model evidence motivates two separate questions: excess mTOR activity with functional impairment, or impaired flux and regeneration. Neither is established here. Everolimus does not replace BUBR1.

### Slide 3 / 0:46–1:13 / Evidence that can change our minds

Evidence cuts both ways. Everolimus improved selected muscle-mass outcomes in aged rats without established force benefit, and reduced BUBR1 in late-passage stromal cells. A randomized RMS trial did not establish benefit from adding temsirolimus. Different compounds and tissues establish neither rescue nor inevitable harm for our pair.

### Slide 4 / 1:13–1:38 / The experiment

First qualify endogenous models, comparing single alleles, cis, trans and corrected cells. Lock one mechanistic branch before treatment. Then randomize a blinded benefit-and-harm probe. Only a worthwhile result advances to independently planned confirmation. If the model or mechanism fails qualification, we revise it before advancing the drug.

### Slide 5 / 1:38–2:02 / What counts as improvement

Count every enrolled cell. In this synthetic example, errors among completed divisions decrease, but accurate viable output falls from sixty to forty percent. That is not rescue. Track death, arrest, missing cells, daughter survival and tissue function. Tumour killing has a separate objective and requires deficient-normal controls.

### Slide 6 / 2:02–2:23 / Stop rules

Unknown evidence means hold. Failed safety stops advancement despite apparent benefit. Require replicated function, preserved division and a justified exposure comparison. Whole blood is not free tissue. A disputed source concentration stays quarantined. All requirements passing would permit preclinical review, not treatment.

### Slide 7 / 2:23–2:46 / Impact and reuse

The value is a decision that survives attempts to disprove it. A validated positive or negative result could guide MVA research. Our contribution combines genotype-aware models, complete cell-fate accounting and explicit stopping rules. Public CPU checks need no subject files or GPUs. New tissues still require fresh biological qualification.
