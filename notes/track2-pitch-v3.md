## Narration

Our first Track 1 entry achieved owner-reported scores of one hundred rank points and
F-max one. That identifies a competition starting point, not a medicine. Our own
genomic analysis still leaves trans phase unconfirmed. Track 2 asks a harder question:
can we improve cellular function, or kill a tumour, without harming non-cancer cells
that share the underlying vulnerability?

BUB1B encodes BUBR1, which helps control chromosome attachment and separation. We
hypothesize impaired function, but have not measured these alleles' exact effects.
AlphaGenome Atlas supplied additional computational scores. They reuse existing
annotation signals, do not establish drug response, and cannot replace RNA, protein
or functional experiments. Small predicted splicing effects do not prove normal splicing.

Our first conditional experiment tests everolimus only if the relevant genotype shows
excessive mTOR signalling. Hydroxychloroquine remains reserve. Approval for another
condition is not evidence of benefit here, and no candidate has a measured clinical
exposure margin. Whole-blood concentrations cannot simply become cell-culture targets.

The extended review made the proposal more demanding. A rapamycin experiment showed
that stressed cells can escape mitotic arrest without dividing normally. We therefore
must track division, death, slippage and subsequent survival, not just count fewer
abnormal mitoses among survivors. That experiment does not prove clinical everolimus harm.

A newer pralatrexate study provides a tumour-only horizon for fusion-positive
rhabdomyosarcoma. Some mouse tumours regressed, but recurrence and schedule-dependent
toxicity prevent calling this a cure or safe combination. Tumour subtype is unknown
here. Short-term drug potency also disagreed with colony suppression in another study:
the smallest number was not automatically the best candidate.

We propose independent engineered clones, single-allele and cis/trans controls, and
genetic correction. Then measure pathway activity, accurate division and tissue function
before drug screening. Randomize treatment, blind scoring and retain complete cell
trajectories. Cells from one well are not independent experiments. Reject apparent
constitutional rescue caused only by arrest or selective death. Across both arms, stop
for unattainable exposure, failed replication or deficient-normal injury. No such
experiments have been performed.

Our contribution is a reproducible, falsifiable research plan, including evidence
against our preferred ideas. Public-source retrieval tools broadened the review; they
did not validate a treatment. The report discloses OpenAI, Google DeepMind and
Fireworks-hosted model use. We thank the child, family, organizers and partners for
making this research possible. There is no treatment recommendation or guaranteed result.
