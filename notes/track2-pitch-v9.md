## Narration

### Slide 1 / 0:00–0:27 / The question

Can everolimus support tissue function in mosaic variegated aneuploidy, or MVA?
Our starting point is BUB1B, the gene for BUBR1, which helps control chromosome
attachment and division timing. We propose a bounded test of benefit and harm in
non-cancer cells. Everolimus is a mechanistic hypothesis, not a preferred rescue drug.

### Slide 2 / 0:27–1:11 / The conflicting evidence

The evidence points in two directions. Mouse muscle carrying different BubR1 variants
showed increased mTORC1 signaling, but drug rescue was not tested. A separate human
stromal-cell study found that everolimus reduced BUBR1 protein in late-passage cells.
Neither experiment tests our selected variants. Higher pathway activity could also
be protective, so suppressing it is not automatically beneficial. We would first
qualify the model, then measure function, BUBR1 and cell fate together. Protein loss
alone does not prove harm, just as pathway suppression does not prove rescue.

### Slide 3 / 1:11–1:39 / The tumour boundary

The tumour trial answers a separate question. In ARST1431, adding temsirolimus to
chemotherapy did not establish an event-free survival benefit among 297 evaluable
participants. The hazard ratio was zero point eight six, with a confidence interval
crossing one. This neither proves zero effect nor tests everolimus for non-cancer
tissue function. Tumour evidence cannot substitute for that experiment.

### Slide 4 / 1:39–2:20 / The experiment

Compare everolimus with vehicle in deficient and corrected models, including cis,
trans, single-variant and wild-type controls. Engineered phase does not resolve the
child's phase. Randomize treatment and blind scoring. Measure tissue function and
track BUBR1, autophagy and every enrolled cell: first-division accuracy, death,
arrest and slippage. Follow daughter survival and report tracking loss separately.
Losing abnormal cells must not be mistaken for functional rescue. An independent
pathway perturbation would help test the mechanism.

### Slide 5 / 2:20–3:00 / The decision

Advance only with functional benefit, acceptable safety, justified exposure and
replication across clones and days. Unexplained BUBR1 loss pauses advancement;
failed functional or safety criteria stop it. No drug currently earns a rescue
priority. Phase and clinical exposure margins remain unresolved, and no experiments
have been performed. We disclose OpenAI, Google DeepMind and Fireworks GLM assistance;
Fireworks training and retention remain unverified. We thank the child, family and
organizers for making this research possible.
