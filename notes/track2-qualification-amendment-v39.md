contract. This amends their interpretation and next experiments; it does not add
independent studies, change the eleven drug dispositions or report new assays.

This cycle explicitly challenges mechanism, model validity, functional endpoints,
failed studies, exposure, safety and alternatives. It uses the retained v21-v36
counterevidence plus the focused October 19 owner-rehearsal source checks. It is not
a new systematic search or an independent outside review. Favorable and unfavorable
findings have the same context, attribution and endpoint requirements.

Everolimus remains an optional mechanistic probe on HOLD for unmeasured prerequisites;
HCQ remains reserve. NAD/SIRT2 is a competing hypothesis, not a newly promoted drug.
The first experiment concerns endogenous function and reversibility. No result in
this amendment establishes phase, clinical exposure, drug efficacy or wet-lab success.
