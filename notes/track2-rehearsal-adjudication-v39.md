# Adjudication of the owner's scientific rehearsal

19 October 2026. The owner supplied a hard rehearsal answering the five v38
comparison questions. This is an AI-assisted check of that argument against primary
sources and retained evidence. It is not independent outside review, an assay result
or treatment advice. The outside-review response fields remain blank.

The rehearsal identifies an important omission in our compressed mouse rationale.
It also introduces errors that would make the proposed alternative less defensible.
The revised proposal starts with endogenous function and compares conditional routes.
No drug earns rescue priority. Everolimus advancement remains **HOLD because its
prerequisites are unmeasured**, not STOP on the strength of an unperformed experiment.

## Findings and decisions

| Rehearsal claim | Decision and correction |
|---|---|
| The mTORC1 evidence needs genotype and phenotype qualifiers. | **Accepted.** Sieben Figure 5 concerns skeletal muscle from heterozygous BubR1+/X753 mice, compared with +/L1002P and wild type. The signal was measured in young adult mice and discussed in relation to later sarcopenia. It is not demonstrated compound-heterozygous rescue or a segregation endpoint. Add the qualifiers to the report, comparison, slide and narration. |
| Established everolimus pharmacology strengthens the causal case. | **Rejected as a causal inference.** It identifies a way to perturb a target. It supplies no evidence that the target drives the retained pair's phenotype. Our compressed table should separate these roles. |
| The signal is probably compensatory because it accompanies aging/senescence. | **Unresolved.** Neither correlation nor a senescence association identifies the direction of causation. The paper itself proposes a possible driver role. Test pathogenic and adaptive explanations symmetrically. |
| SIRT2 survival benefit is sex dependent. | **Accepted and sharpened.** North reports increased median lifespan overall and in males, with no median-lifespan change in females. Separate this genetic experiment from NMN abundance and niacin studies. It does not predict response in this subject. |
| K668, K250 and K243 are three distinct sites. | **Corrected.** Mouse K243 corresponds to human K250. North identifies human K668 as a separate stability site. Qiu additionally tests mouse K657, but its text uses human 688 in one passage and 668 elsewhere; retain that inconsistency rather than silently repairing it. The three numbers in the rehearsal do not establish three distinct sites in one protein. |
| K243R is the cleanest positive control for somatic rescue. | **Rejected.** Qiu's favorable result is in mouse oocyte meiosis. Park found weakened checkpoint maintenance, chromosome missegregation and spontaneous tumors in K243R/+ mice. Both findings matter; a meiotic result cannot qualify a human somatic positive control. |
| NAD/SIRT2 should outrank mTOR because it is closer to BUBR1. | **Partly accepted.** It is a more proximal hypothesis for residual-protein regulation and deserves equal examination. Proximity does not establish useful mutant function, drug specificity, an eligible approved intervention or safety. No drug-ranking promotion follows. |
| SIRT2 overexpression is an orthogonal way to prove that more BUBR1 rescues function. | **Corrected.** SIRT2 has other substrates and can change checkpoint behavior without changing total BUBR1. Use independently controlled BUBR1 correction/restoration to test attribution; treat SIRT2 as a separate perturbation. A correlation between abundance and function alone does not prove mediation. |
| A single sample set and an extra blot resolve the routes. | **Narrowed.** This is a multi-readout qualification panel. Mitotic index mixes entry, arrest, escape and death; localization does not establish complex function. Total/modified protein and phosphoprotein assays need validated specificity and appropriate cell-state controls. Site-specific reagents or mass spectrometry are not assumed available. |
| A negative mTOR measurement leaves no rationale for everolimus. | **Narrowed.** A valid, sufficiently precise negative prerequisite can close the tested excess-mTOR branch in that context. Missing or imprecise measurements mean HOLD. The separately justified flux branch cannot be substituted after seeing a negative result. Neither result proves universal drug inefficacy. |
| A useful downstream modifier is defensible only if BUBR1 cannot be restored. | **Rejected.** Downstream mitigation can be useful without repairing the initiating defect. It still needs direct functional benefit and injury measurements. Exhausting every restoration strategy is not a logical prerequisite. |
| Per-division error scoring fixes survivor bias. | **Partly accepted.** It is essential but conditions on entering/completing division. Retain all enrolled cells, nondivision, death, slippage, missing tracks, daughter fate and useful output. Falling error fraction with falling output rejects a benefit claim; it does not by itself prove which adverse mechanism caused the pattern. |
| Patient fibroblasts or lymphoblastoid lines are feasible before the deadline. | **Not established.** No sample, line, laboratory or timeline is available. EBV-immortalized lymphoblastoid lines also need explicit transformation controls. The revised first model is a proposed engineered hTERT-RPE1 qualification comparator, not an available patient model or a renal/muscle surrogate. |
| Everolimus carries a boxed warning, based on a molecule database flag. | **Product scope corrected.** Zortress has a transplant boxed warning. The inspected Afinitor/Afinitor Disperz label has serious warnings and precautions without the same boxed-warning section. Cite the product and indication; a ChEMBL flag or USAN stem cannot establish the proposed use's risk-benefit. |
| Drug repurposing is an overstatement without demonstrated rescue. | **Partly accepted.** Calling a drug effective would be unsupported. A clearly conditional approved-drug hypothesis with a discriminating preclinical plan fits the proposal track. Present qualification and the worked example prominently, without replacing the drug rationale with software claims alone. |

## What changes now

The first qualification question is whether the engineered endogenous deficit is
reproducible and functionally reversible. The candidate model is hTERT-RPE1, chosen
as a nonmalignant, editable comparator already represented in our public analysis.
It is immortalized and the retained expression result is weakly specific; both are
limitations. Clone feasibility, karyotype and checkpoint/function qualification
remain unmeasured. No new patient sample or completed experiment is implied.

The minimum readout panel distinguishes residual BUBR1, checkpoint maintenance,
unchallenged division and chromosome errors, and subsequent viable output/recovery.
Independent biological cultures are the experimental units; individual cells are
nested observations. Expression-matched restoration and independent perturbation
test attribution before pharmacologic interpretation. Tissue-specific functional
models are later work. The [laboratory handoff](track2-lab-handoff-v39.md) defines
what would stop, hold or reopen each branch without inventing a deadline or dose.

The presentation now treats everolimus as a worked conditional branch awaiting
qualification. NAD/SIRT2 receives its proximal rationale and its opposing somatic
evidence. Neither route is declared qualified. The offline demo's current HOLD
state, injury precedence and preclinical-only all-pass result remain unchanged.
