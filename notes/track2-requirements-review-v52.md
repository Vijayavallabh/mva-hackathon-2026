# Track 2 requirements and latest-thread refresh, v52

22 October 2026. This is an anonymous public-source refresh, not a contact,
authenticated account check, or submission preflight. It rechecked the official
Track 2 files, methods template, discussion listing, and the three most
requirement-relevant recent threads (31, 32, and 33).

The official Track 2 brief remains unchanged: identify existing, approved
medications that are plausible candidates for further investigation, based on the
biological mechanisms disrupted in this case. Submissions are hypotheses for follow-up,
not evidence that a medicine works. The written report must characterize the variant
mechanism—including loss-of-function/gain-of-function direction, disrupted pathway,
and downstream biological consequence—and use it as the basis for the repurposing
rationale. The panel rubric remains scientific rigor 35%, potential impact 25%,
innovation 25%, and scalability 15%. The submission limit remains three entries, with
only the latest reviewed. The video remains a three-minute YouTube/Vimeo pitch. The
report remains PDF or Markdown with the username/team name in the filename.

The six official submission files and methods template remain byte-identical to the
v42 capture. Thread 33 is an open README/SDK update pull request; it changes no Track 2
requirement. Thread 32 contains an organizer reply permitting a written report that
covers the challenge case. Thread 31 documents a Synapse alternative for
GitHub-constrained teams; our public GitHub route remains accepted and selected.

No new organizer requirement appeared. This refresh does not establish video hosting,
provider settings, quota, eligibility, upload readiness, biological validation, or
prize outcome. Raw public receipts are in `sources/public-official-v52/`.
