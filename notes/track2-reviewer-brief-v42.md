# The proposal in one page, version 42

**Proposal.** Qualify endogenous BUB1B function before advancing a drug hypothesis.
Everolimus, an approved mTORC1 inhibitor, is a worked conditional branch on HOLD for
missing prerequisites. No drug earns rescue priority; HCQ remains reserve.

**Why the qualification matters.** The retained pair's phase and endogenous effects
are unresolved. The mTORC1 mouse signal comes from heterozygous BubR1+/X753 skeletal
muscle with sarcopenia relevance. It does not establish the pathway state or a
rapalog response in this pair. NAD/SIRT2 offers a more proximal stability hypothesis,
but genetic male-biased survival is distinct from precursor-drug efficacy. Mouse
K243 corresponds to human K250; the favorable oocyte mutant result has opposing
somatic checkpoint/tumor evidence. Neither route is qualified.

**First proposed experiment.** Use engineered hTERT-RPE1 as a qualification comparator,
with wild type/mock, single-allele, cis/trans and corrected controls. The system is
immortalized and its retained expression specificity is weak. Availability and
functional qualification are unconfirmed. Independent, expression-matched BUBR1
restoration tests attribution; SIRT2 overexpression is a separate perturbation.
Measure residual protein, checkpoint maintenance, unchallenged chromosome errors,
all-enrolled fates and subsequent useful output/recovery. The current literature
extension adds BUBR1/MAD2/ZW10 corona localization, MCC/APC/C engagement,
mitochondrial integrity and proteostasis stress. No patient sample or completed
experiment is implied.

**Then test a qualified branch.** Drug plus deficit must be compared with each alone
and corrected controls. Use independent cultures, randomized blocks, blinded scoring
and fresh confirmation. Unknowns hold; valid futility stops the tested claim; injury
stops advancement. All-pass permits preclinical review only. Clinical exposure
margins remain unknown.

**Specific contribution.** Link evidence states and all-enrolled outcomes to an
inspectable next laboratory decision. The demo exposes exact inputs and HOLD/STOP
reasons; the handoff identifies the measurement that can change them. This is an
integration claim, not a claim that the component methods are new.

**What exists today.** Auditable public model findings and counterevidence, a
conditional experimental plan, and an offline demo of 17 synthetic cases plus the
actual unmeasured HOLD state. The method distinguishes a lower error fraction from
higher useful output. Software checks establish implementation, not biological
validity or external adoption.
