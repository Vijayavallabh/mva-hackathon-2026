# Track 2 rubric self-assessment

This is a self-assessment against the official 35/25/25/15 rubric. It is not a prediction of placement, prize eligibility, or organizer judgment. It states what is supported and where the package remains limited.

## Scientific Rigor — 35%

**Supported strengths**

- The report characterizes the BUB1B/BUBR1 gene-level mechanism, states that the selected pair’s endogenous effect and phase are unmeasured, and does not substitute gene-level evidence for pair-specific function.
- A prespecified N1002K classification gate separates abundance, turnover, HSP90/proteasome response and function at equalized expression before any stabilization or drug inference.
- The proposed four-arm factorial experiment is genotype-controlled: deficient/corrected × vehicle/everolimus.
- The primary endpoint is useful output per enrolled founder cell, with death, arrest, slippage and missingness retained rather than discarded.
- Injury, exposure and failed assay controls have explicit stop/hold consequences.
- Computational work is tied to decisions and abandonment conditions, not model agreement.
- No blind retrospective benchmark, calibrated drug score or clinical exposure margin is claimed.

**Honest limits**

- No wet-lab experiment has been performed.
- The proposed hTERT-RPE1 comparator is immortalized, has weak retained expression specificity, and its availability and qualification are unconfirmed.
- Direct mTORC1 state in the relevant model is unmeasured.
- The carrier-mouse evidence is from another allele and tissue.

## Potential Impact — 25%

**Supported strengths**

- If independently validated, the allele-classification and four-arm design could improve mechanistic characterization of BUB1B variants and prevent unsupported routes from consuming scarce material.
- A valid negative result is a usable outcome because it stops a tested branch with a recorded reason.
- The plain-language family summary makes the proposal accessible to patient advocates without adding claims.
- The testability/go-no-go matrix prevents an uninterpretable positive result.

**Honest limits**

- Neither a positive nor negative result establishes benefit for this child.
- No family co-design or external biological validation is claimed.
- Clinical exposure margins remain null.

## Innovation — 25%

**Supported strengths**

- The useful-output denominator distinguishes genuine cellular output from reduced division, selective survival and missing observations.
- The offline Every-enrolled-cell demo makes HOLD/STOP states inspectable without subject files, GPUs, model weights or network.
- The reuse qualification contract separates decision-layer transferability from biological assumptions that do not transfer automatically.
- Candidate selection is a five-criterion biological argument, not an opaque numerical index.

**Honest limits**

- Component methods are established; broad novelty and superior clinical performance are not claimed.
- The teaching cases are author-selected failure modes, not an external benchmark.
- Software verification does not validate biological thresholds.

## Scalability — 15%

**Supported strengths**

- The integrated public review runs on CPU without subject files, data/results folders, keys, network or GPUs.
- The schema, decision contract and replayable checks can be instantiated for another supported disease gene after the four-part qualification contract is met.
- The family summary and lab handoff are reusable communication artifacts.

**Honest limits**

- Other tissues, genotypes and diseases need new mechanism, model, functional endpoint, exposure and safety qualification.
- This is not an automated gene-to-drug candidate-generation engine.
- No measured community adoption or independent biological review is claimed.

## Overall boundary

No result in this package establishes treatment efficacy, phase, clinical exposure margin, rescue priority or benefit for this child. Everolimus remains a conditional approved probe on HOLD pending the stated prerequisites.
