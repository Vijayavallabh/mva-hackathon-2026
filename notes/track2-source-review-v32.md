# Sources for the v32 integration

V32 integrates the frozen v31 public Perturb-seq result and its bounded source review.
The source data, query records, article reading depth, failed requests and decisions
are in [the source record](track2-perturbseq-sources-v31.json) and
[primary-source review](track2-perturbseq-source-review-v31.md). All 98 returned titles
were screened; selected abstracts/passages were read. This is not a systematic review
or an independent full-paper review.

The main report carries Replogle/Weissman data provenance, missing-control sensitivity,
guide dependence and differences between RPE1 and K562. It also carries the clinical
comparison limits for BIOMEDE and TEAMMATE and the yeast-only scope of the 2026 TORC1
cohesin finding. No drug or clinical exposure margin changes.

Official requirements and discussion coverage retain the October 6 source review,
revision `c9b4a7e6247574124cd5bbfd249af9af6f1bbac3`, 26 threads and 79 visible comments.
The v32 JSON copies preserve that retrieval provenance; they are not a fresh full
community review. An October 7 top-level Space request succeeded, but supplied only
the wrapper. DOI/PMC browser requests for Replogle and BIOMEDE encountered provider
errors or browser challenges; no new reading claim is based on them. The previously
retrieved v31 primary text supports the integration. Final live portal checks remain
separate from this editing task.

Historical [v30 source review](track2-source-review-v30.md),
[official review](track2-requirements-review-v30.md) and all earlier source ledgers
remain unchanged. No organizer or family contact occurred.
