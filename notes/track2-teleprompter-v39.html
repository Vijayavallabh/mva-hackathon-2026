Slide 1 · 0:00-0:18 · Qualify before advancing

A drug must improve useful function without worsening chromosome instability. Our contribution is a qualification experiment and a public decision tool. Everolimus is a worked conditional branch awaiting evidence. This is a research hypothesis; no drug earns rescue priority.

Slide 2 · 0:18-0:40 · Function before the drug branch

BUBR1 controls chromosome attachment and division. This pair’s phase and endogenous effects remain unresolved. The mTOR signal came from carrier mouse muscle with sarcopenia relevance. Neither branch is established. NAD and SIRT2 offer another hypothesis, with sex and cell-context limits. Everolimus does not replace BUBR1.

Slide 3 · 0:40-1:03 · The decisive experiment

First qualify engineered RPE1 cells with independent perturbation and restoration. Availability and useful function are unconfirmed. Then compare drug plus deficit with each alone and corrected controls. Randomize cultures, blind scoring and confirm independently. Measure chromosome errors, all enrolled fates, useful output and recovery.

Slide 4 · 1:03-1:27 · Demonstrate the hidden failure

This example is synthetic. Errors fall from twenty-five to eleven percent, but accurate viable output drops from sixty to forty per hundred enrolled cells. Even favorable missing-observation assignments cannot reverse the decline. Track every cell and later daughter fate; early relative sensitivity can reverse.

Slide 5 · 1:27-1:46 · Evidence against the hypothesis

ProteinMPNN failed expanded functional controls. Repeatable RPE1 expression was weakly specific. Separate drug profiles cannot establish rescue. Sirolimus plus exercise did not establish functional benefit in forty older adults. Direct function and injury measurements must decide whether a branch advances.

Slide 6 · 1:46-2:06 · What imaging contributes

Simple public HeLa profiles place BUB1B among the top three genes for all four interphase guides. One guide varies by method and plate. Neural models miss the required gain, and mitotic coverage is insufficient. This is an assay-development lead, not established non-cancer rescue.

Slide 7 · 2:06-2:29 · Demonstrate the decision tool

Our offline demo explains eighteen evidence cases. Invalid assays hold inference; imprecision holds advancement. Injury stops advancement despite apparent benefit. Whole blood is not free tissue: clinical exposure remains unknown. Passing everything permits preclinical review only. No wet-lab experiment has been performed.

Slide 8 · 2:29-2:48 · Impact and community use

Other teams can inspect the examples and replay the rules without subject files or GPUs. New tissues need fresh qualification. We propose engineered models, without requesting another family sample. HCQ remains reserve. The contribution survives a failed drug hypothesis, while every new route needs evidence.
