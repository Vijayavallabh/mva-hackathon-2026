# Track 2 v24 presentation design and inspection

The judging panel needs an approved-drug hypothesis, the evidence that challenges it,
and a discriminating next experiment within a three-minute pitch. V24 adds a dedicated
RNAi slide to the established visual sequence. The narration is 342 words; its nine
cue timings are rehearsal allocations, not measured runtime.

Keep ice #f7fafc, ink #142c3c, deep green #123a40, green #176359, violet #48419d and
muted blue #4c6370, with pale mint/lilac for grouping. Ubuntu is the sole slide family.
Left-aligned text, 64px outer margins and local SVG figures support a scientific talk.
The mitotic spindle remains the opening image. Color separates observations and
conditions; it does not encode efficacy or safety.

```text
1  Everolimus hypothesis / conceptual mitosis
2  Genetic uncertainty / two separately qualified mechanism branches
3  Earlier query filter / compound identity and QC
4  Seed-associated similarity / HT29 finite-reference sensitivity
5  Primary human functional estimate / differing animal findings
6  Qualify -> joint intervention -> independent confirmation
7  Five evidence states / distinct actions
8  Functional test and reuse
9  Complete acknowledgement
```

Slide 4 uses two labeled numerical comparisons. A decorative molecular image would
not explain the new finding. The 45/54 denominator is evaluable reagent-context
records, shown separately for each representation; the right column labels the fixed
and post-hoc adjusted reference tails. The unchanged effect and changing comparison
family are explained in the report and recording notes. The six-reagent PRIME result
has its own slide to avoid conflating it with v19's five-reagent projection.

All nine screenshots were inspected in a contact sheet and slide 4 at full resolution.
The final deck has no detected text clipping or overlap, minimum 24px slide text,
640px narrow-screen fit and minimum checked contrast 5.257:1. The full acknowledgement
is visible. Nine report PDF pages were inspected; all four tables and the acknowledgement
remain intact. The workbook was checked by round-trip values/styles, not visual rendering.
Versioned audits bind the inspected outputs. No image-generation service or independent
visual reviewer was used.
