# Track 2 v30 visual plan

The judging panel needs a drug hypothesis, the evidence that limits it and a decisive
experiment within three minutes. Nine slides retain the mitotic-spindle opening and
give the new structural and held-out expression findings separate visual space.

The palette is ice #f7fafc, ink #142c3c, deep green #123a40, green #176359,
violet #48419d and muted blue #4c6370. Ubuntu supplies headings, labels and body text.
Use 64px margins, left alignment and large local SVG figures. The spindle provides
identity; the evidence figures show measured comparisons with explicit denominators.
Colors distinguish analyses, not efficacy or safety.

```text
1  Approved-drug hypothesis / conceptual mitosis
2  Protein control failures / separate stability, scaffolding and catalysis
3  HT29 QC-restricted agreement / before adjustment and held-out range
4  MCF7 reversal weakens / qualified BUB1B query is missing
5  Human functional uncertainty / differing animal results
6  Qualify -> joint intervention -> independent confirmation
7  Evidence state -> decision
8  Useful function, complete fate and bounded injury / reuse
9  Complete acknowledgement
```

Slide 2 uses a control-comparison diagram rather than a protein rendering: predicted
folds did not qualify function. Slide 3 shows a zero-based correlation axis and an
explicit sensitivity range, which is not a confidence interval. Slide 4 separates
drug QC from query QC, retains MTOR evidence and keeps the held-out rank denominators
next to the values. Neither figure implies joint treatment or independent replication.

This plan was checked against the competition brief before implementation. Retaining
the scientific visual identity gives continuity across versions. Large compute counts
belong in the methods record; the deck emphasizes what changed the next experiment.
Local SVG/HTML rendering needs no image-generation provider. Final visual inspection
and corrections are recorded in the integration review after rendering.
