# Track 2 v36 presentation design

Audience: the Track 2 expert panel. Purpose: assess an approved-drug research
hypothesis, the evidence that could reject it, and the next preclinical decision.
Scientific-slides, frontend-design, humanizer and no-ai-slop inform this revision.
No external image generator or additional model provider is needed for these local
SVG figures. Visual and editorial checks will be recorded after rendering.

The previous deck devoted three consecutive slides to computational limitations.
This revision puts the mechanism and proposed joint-treatment experiment first.
One evidence slide and one optical result slide show how falsification changed the
plan. Detailed scores and every failed control remain in linked research records.

Palette: ice #f2f7f8, ink #142c3c, deep teal #123a40, mint #d8eee5,
iris #48419d and muted blue #425c6b. Ubuntu carries both headings and body text.
Headings use 51-90 px; chart/body labels use at least 24 px. The figures use shape,
position and text as well as color. Avoid decorative gradients, repeated cards and
an unrelated biological illustration.

Layout: fixed 1280 by 720, one claim per slide, consistent source/footer placement.
The memorable visual is a cell-outcome diagram comparing the denominator of a
surviving-cell ratio with the full enrolled population. It is explicitly synthetic.
The mechanism uses a causal hypothesis diagram with unresolved links labelled;
the experiment uses a two-by-two treatment matrix; the optical comparison uses
actual BUB1B guide ranks beside global method performance and the prespecified threshold. No fabricated molecular
structure, model-success illustration or implied clinical trajectory.

| Slide | Message | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Test everolimus for useful non-cancer function | Conceptual spindle and chromosome illustration |
| 2 | Establish the proposed mechanism before exposure | BUBR1 and two conditional pathway branches |
| 3 | Measure drug plus deficit against each alone | Randomized two-by-two experiment and restoration control |
| 4 | Count every enrolled cell and later output | Labelled synthetic denominator example |
| 5 | Contrary evidence changed the plan | Endpoint-specific biological and model controls |
| 6 | Report corrected optical results against strong baselines | Actual held-out performance and query coverage |
| 7 | Different failures require different decisions | Invalid, inconclusive, futile, unsafe and all-pass states |
| 8 | Make the next decision reproducible | Public CPU review and staged biological qualification |
| 9 | Required acknowledgement | Full unmodified acknowledgement |

The static source must pass the restricted HTML/SVG parser. Render every slide
locally with networking and page scripts blocked; inspect all nine PNGs. Check
contrast, text bounds, overlaps, narrow layout and exported PDF pages. Narrative
runtime stays unmeasured until a recording exists. Rehearsal allocations alone do
not establish the required three-minute video.
