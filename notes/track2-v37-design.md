# V37 presentation and interaction design

Audience: researchers, clinicians and patient advocates evaluating a three-minute
Track 2 pitch without live Q&A. Scientific-slides and frontend-design guide the
visual work; humanizer/no-ai-slop guide the prose. No image-generation provider is used.

Keep the established ice/teal/mint/iris palette and Ubuntu typography. The nine-slide
deck retains eight vector figures, with a clearer primary branch, a bounded imaging
lead and an explicit offline review-tool contribution. The last slide retains the
complete required acknowledgement. Source links remain visible. The final exports
must pass geometry, embedded-font and contrast checks and be visually inspected.

The demo uses a 100-cell outcome grid, explicit denominators, missingness controls
and readable decision labels. Native selects/buttons, associated labels, visible
focus, text-plus-color, responsive reflow and no automatic animation support access.
It embeds its inputs and script and sends no network requests. Downloaded examples
remain local. The actual unmeasured state is the default; synthetic examples stay
labelled. “Stop advancement; review injury” replaces an ambiguous machine-like label.

The owner chose their own voice. The teleprompter uses the 368-word final narration,
contiguous cue allocations totaling 180 seconds, pause/reset and keyboard controls.
It captures no audio, requests no microphone permission and supplies no proof of
recording duration. The final exported recording must be measured separately.

Browser checks inspect desktop/mobile widths, controls, all 18 case selections,
download contents, reset focus and cue boundaries. They are not a formal accessibility
certification or an external usability study. Visual inspection here means this agent’s review of rendered images, not an
independent or external reviewer.
