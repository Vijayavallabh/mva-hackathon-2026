## Material refinements relative to the previous synthesis

Two positive/negative details deserve addition, without changing prioritization:

1. **Make the mTOR evidence smaller and more exact.** The original phosphoprotein
   panel, replication size and unblinded/nonrandomized Methods support a limited
   association. A future assay can include phospho-S6, but must label it as proposed.
   Drug suppression of a pathway marker alone does not establish that the pathway
   drives a harmful phenotype or that suppression improves function.
2. **Preserve the actual positive PR65 binding result.** Prior notes emphasize
   optical-tweezer mechanics and correctly dispute the phrase “single-molecule
   binding affinity.” They should not be paraphrased as absence of all binding
   measurements: the new review verifies ensemble fluorescence polarization.
   Binding to isolated PR65 still does not establish the disputed compound's
   cellular target, kinetochore localization, approved-drug status or rescue.

The source observations underlying these refinements are recorded in the table;
their implications for the proposed design are reviewer interpretation.

Other previously adjudicated corrections remain mandatory: do not conflate MVA
localization rescue with the HeLa chemical experiment; reverse p21's tissue effects;
discard real receptor-function controls because they were missing from a supplied
abstract; call temsirolimus simply inactive; or reject entinostat on a worldwide
nonapproval claim. The older source notes preserve useful favorable observations
as well as negative results. This rereview does not erase them.

## Recommendations for v4 integration

These are design/synthesis recommendations, not completed experiments:

- Keep a compact lead/benchmark/reserve/horizon/exclusion comparison. Every row
  needs its model, favorable observation, strongest limitation and reopening gate;
  do not create an efficacy score from the number of papers or model passes.
- State the mTOR hypothesis as **testable downstream mitigation**, not direct
  BUBR1 repair. Do not require that an actionable secondary driver be uniquely
  genotype-specific or appear before every other defect. Do require reproducible
  excess activity in the selected model and improvement of a prespecified function.
- Separate pathway engagement, chromosome-segregation correction and tissue
  function. Downstream improvement without restored segregation may still be
  biologically useful, but must not be labelled chromosome repair; segregation
  and other normal-cell safety outcomes remain explicit guardrails.
- Enroll cells before treatment, retain death/arrest/slippage trajectories and
  censoring, and measure accurate completed divisions plus daughter survival.
  A smaller abnormal fraction among survivors is not sufficient. Zero observed
  harm needs a detection limit and uncertainty interval; it is not proof of zero risk.
- Keep deficient-normal and corrected controls alongside tumour controls. Do not
  accept healthy-myoblast selectivity alone, a lower metabolic-dye signal, or
  necrosis without sustained tumour control as the desired therapeutic outcome.
- A failed mechanistic rescue construct only informs target attribution after
  verifying its expression and functional restoration. Even then it does not
  prove that every remaining drug action is nonspecific toxicity.
- Set exposure ceilings only after pharmacology review matching formulation,
  active species, matrix, binding and time course. Ratios of nominal culture
  potency to total or estimated free plasma do not establish a clinical margin.
- Preserve the oncology supplement's compound-specific findings and exceptions;
  do not turn the China approval correction into an automatic entinostat promotion,
  or the negative rapalog trial into direct refutation of constitutional benefit.
- A short pitch should communicate the decisive uncertainty and falsifiable
  experiment, not compress ten dossiers into claims of ten candidate therapies.
  State no performed wet-lab work, unknown clinical margin and unconfirmed trans.

The following refinements were sent to the main author before final freeze:

1. **Avoid making segregation correction compulsory for every downstream hypothesis.**
   The proposed fraction producing accurate viable daughter pairs is reasonable for
   a qualified division-defect model, but not necessarily sensitive to an mTOR-related
   lineage-function deficit. Suggested heading: “Candidate primary endpoint for a
   qualified division-defect model.” Suggested additional text:

   > For an mTOR-associated lineage-function deficit without a demonstrable baseline
   > division deficit, prespecify the relevant lineage-function endpoint before
   > screening. Improvement with unchanged segregation is downstream functional
   > mitigation, not chromosome repair; division accuracy/capacity and all fate
   > outcomes remain mandatory safety guardrails.

   This is proposed endpoint-selection logic, not permission for post-treatment
   outcome switching or dismissal of drug-induced segregation injury.
2. **Separate a first division from subsequent daughter survival.** In validation
   section 5, the first-event row combined accurate division with viable daughters,
   potentially leaving accurate divisions followed by daughter death without an
   explicit first-event class. Record accurate/error-containing division as the first
   event and daughter viability as follow-up. Derive the useful-division composite
   without discarding late-death or missing-daughter tracks. The existing clustered
   analysis, competing fates, enrollment denominator and missingness plan are retained.
3. **Calibrate the optional PP2A control claim.** An alignment-only rescue can qualify
   an alignment assay or recruitment mechanism; it need not already prove complete
   functional restoration to be a useful mechanistic control. Suggested wording:
   “Alignment improvement qualifies only that endpoint; claiming full segregation
   rescue additionally requires accurate completed divisions and viable daughters.”
   This does not promote a chemical treatment or erase the published arrest condition.
4. **Retain positive PR65 binding without overpromoting it.** The report's term
   “isolated-PR65 biophysics” is not false, but “isolated-PR65 binding/stabilization”
   is more informative and consistent with the newly checked ensemble assay. This
   remains a different-compound/scaffold observation, not a DT-061 mechanism resolution.

These refinements concern prospective estimands and interpretation, not a change
in research priority. The pitch, machine-readable decision ledger and final revised
versions were not yet available for this initial cross-document check; they require
their own consistency check rather than an assumed pass.
