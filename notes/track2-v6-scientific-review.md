## Story and evidence boundaries

The useful story is one conditional experiment: determine whether a qualified
BUB1B model has excess mTORC1 activity, then test whether everolimus improves a
prespecified non-cancer function at a justified exposure. BUB1B identifies the
biological starting point. The different-allele mouse observation supplies a
limited pathway hypothesis. Neither establishes drug rescue in the selected pair.

| Story element | Required evidence boundary |
|---|---|
| Why BUB1B matters | BUB1B encodes BUBR1 and biallelic disruption is an established MVA mechanism. The selected pair's phase and allele-specific function remain unresolved. |
| Why consider everolimus | Sieben's muscle/genotype comparison measured phospho-p70 S6 kinase and phospho-4EBP1 in different-allele mice. The study did not test rapalog rescue; the depicted blot groups were small and experiments were not randomized or blinded. |
| What could improve | A model-appropriate prespecified function, with division and deficient-normal safety measured alongside it. Downstream functional mitigation need not repair chromosome segregation. |
| What the tumour evidence says | ARST1431 did not demonstrate an EFS benefit from adding temsirolimus to its chemotherapy regimen. HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.58-1.26, p=0.44 and 297 evaluable participants cannot be converted into proof of zero effect or a direct test of constitutional everolimus rescue. |
| What counts as a useful outcome | Follow every enrolled cell, distinguish first division from daughter survival, preserve death/arrest/slippage, and report tracking loss as missing information. A lower abnormal fraction among survivors is insufficient. |
| What stops advancement | Absent target phenotype, no functional benefit, unacceptable deficient-normal injury, unjustified exposure or failure to replicate. Unresolved exposure can leave a mechanistic result but cannot support translational advancement. |
| What remains separate | Tumour efficacy needs a relevant model and matched deficient-normal controls. HCQ stays reserve and pralatrexate stays an optional fusion-positive RMS direction. Neither displaces the sole conditional lead. |
