## 1. Define the claim and what could defeat it

The constitutional question is whether a specified intervention improves a
prespecified functional deficit in a qualified model, without unacceptable injury
to useful division, chromosome fidelity, viability, regeneration or recovery.
This question does not require benefit to be exclusive to the deficient genotype.
Genotype-specific claims additionally require an interaction contrast with corrected
controls; different within-group p-values do not establish that interaction.

Keep the tumour question separate: subtype-appropriate benefit against a justified
oncology comparator, with deficient-normal function assessed at matched exposure.
Tumour killing cannot compensate for normal-tissue injury. Neither question measures
benefit in the child. Engineered phase establishes the construct's phase only.

Before collecting confirmatory outcomes, record the model/background, relevant stage,
intervention, comparator, enrollment denominator, horizon, meaningful benefit, injury
bounds and intended scope. Specify the strongest competing explanation and the
observation that would reject the claim. A negative result can reject a specific
mechanism or exposure schedule without rejecting every possible intervention.

## 2. Establish that the model and measurement can answer the question

Compare parental wild type, mock editing, each heterozygous allele, cis and trans
pair constructs, and precisely corrected derivatives. Verify engineered genotype,
phase, copy number/background karyotype, cell state and relevant editing artifacts.
Use independent clones and genetic perturbation/correction controls. Clone recovery
failure is a selection/feasibility result, not proof of the child's mechanism.

Measure endogenous RNA and protein, full-length/truncated species, turnover and
localization. Ectopic cDNA may bypass RNA decay; published 731X experiments cannot
assign L737Ter function. Separate checkpoint activation, maintenance and silencing
from attachment, accurate division and daughter survival. Protein abundance alone
cannot qualify restoration of useful BUBR1. Expression-matched correction matters:
overexpression can create a different biological state.

Qualify instrument range, controls, interference, segmentation/tracking and blinded
event adjudication. An assay-positive control validates its endpoint only. Alignment
under arrest does not establish later segregation rescue. Failed controls make a
measurement invalid; low precision makes it inconclusive. Neither establishes that
the allele is benign or the disease mechanism absent.

Begin in one tractable non-transformed context. A new background, renal lineage or
muscle developmental stage needs qualification before transfer. Mature myotubes
need a validated function endpoint, such as evoked contraction, rather than a
requirement to divide; link precursor and mature-lineage experiments explicitly.
Do not manufacture senescence or another insult solely to obtain a drug-responsive
phenotype. No model here has yet met these requirements.

## 3. Separate branch eligibility from drug response

**A: excess mTOR-associated activity with a relevant deficit.** Control nutrient,
density, passage, cell-cycle and differentiation state; use total/activated readouts,
adequate dynamic range and corrected comparisons. Excess may be adaptive. Titrated,
time-matched orthogonal pathway perturbation can challenge mediation, with its own
non-targeting/restoration controls. A knockout is not exposure-equivalent to a drug.
Absence of reproducible excess in a qualified test stops A; poor assay performance
holds interpretation. [Mouse rationale](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI126863).

**B: impaired flux with a relevant regenerative/function deficit.** Distinguish
inadequate induction from impaired degradation. Increasing initiation into a blocked
clearance system is not an assumed rescue. Use orthogonal cargo turnover, reporter
expression/pH controls, optical-interference controls and viable-cell counts. Genetic
autophagy perturbation that independently injures cells is not a clean comparator.
Neither branch has been established for this pair. Lock eligibility and endpoint
before treatment; do not relabel failed A as successful B. The Balnis supplemental
10 mM/10 micromolar discrepancy remains quarantined; neither value sets a dose.
[Source and supplement](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.182842).

HCQ is not the sole positive or negative control for autophagy mediation. Its
non-lysosomal chromatin effects require independent function and chromosome-timing
assessment before reopening the reserve candidate. NAD/SIRT2 is a separate exploratory
mechanistic alternative: distinguish NMN, nicotinamide and niacin, and establish
residual useful protein and checkpoint behavior before any drug claim.

## 4. Test the joint intervention and real exposure

Resolve chemical identity and material provenance, including stereochemistry where
relevant. Record formulation, analyte/metabolites, matrix, free fraction, medium/serum,
pH, stability, depletion, concentration-time sampling and carryover. Whole blood,
total plasma, nominal medium and free tissue are distinct. Unknown tissue accumulation
is not a favorable assumption; all current clinical margins remain null.

Measure drug response **in the qualified deficient model**, with vehicle, corrected
comparisons, qualified assay controls and an interpretable orthogonal perturbation.
Drug-only and knockdown-only profiles cannot replace that experiment. Discordant
orthogonal results limit mediation claims without automatically disproving a
well-measured drug-specific effect. Binding or docking alone cannot set direction.

Prespecify graded exposure/time within an authorized laboratory's justified design.
The aim is function, not maximal phosphoprotein suppression. Test precursor activation,
expansion, differentiation and mature function separately; measure delayed effects,
recovery and residual drug. Muscle mass is not force. Schedule-dependent animal and
human findings require this separation; they do not supply a pediatric schedule.
Monitor endogenous BUBR1, relevant feedback/metabolic effects and chromosome function.
An isolated BUBR1 loss pauses advancement for mechanistic review; functional injury
crossing a justified bound stops it. [Goutas](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.redox.2023.102701).

A mechanistic experiment without a human exposure bridge can answer a scoped research
question. It cannot pass the translational advancement gate. This plan specifies
neither a culture concentration nor a dose for the child.

## 5. Randomize the correct unit and retain every outcome

Randomize treatment at the independently treated well/culture level within
clone × day × plate blocks; balance processing order and position. Blind scoring
and prespecify exclusions. Independently prepared wells are experimental units for
their randomized contrast; duplicate measurements are technical repeats. Cells and
daughter cells are nested observations. Clones and culture days supply additional
replication dimensions; several clones from one donor do not create several donors.
Report each level. Crossed clone/day effects and plate effects must appear in the
analysis or justified aggregation; thousands of cells cannot replace missing cultures.

Enroll before exposure. Fix a horizon and retain mutually exclusive first events:
accurate completed division, erroneous division, death, slippage, persistent arrest/
no division, and tracking loss with reason. Follow daughters and later events in a
separate longitudinal table. Do not classify tracking loss automatically as death
or count repeated divisions as new independent units. Include absolute output per
original culture, not only surviving-cell ratios. The preserved synthetic 100-cell
example demonstrates this bias; it is not experimental evidence.

A division-defect endpoint may be the fraction of enrolled cells yielding an accurately
divided viable daughter pair by fixed division and survival horizons. A lineage-function
deficit may instead require a qualified function endpoint. Choose before screening;
retain division/fate safety and later clonal/karyotypic composition where interpretable.
For tumour comparisons, measure direct baseline/longitudinal counts and deficient-normal
function. ATP alone is insufficient; growth-rate normalization needs growing controls.

## 6. Separate discovery from confirmation and quantify uncertainty

Pilot measurements establish reliability, plausible variance/correlation and a
biologically justified benefit target and injury margins. Pilot estimates are uncertain:
plan a sensitivity range rather than selecting an optimistic single effect. Lock the
independent confirmation design, allocation, exclusions, missingness assumptions,
estimands, interval construction/coverage, multiplicity and stopping rules before
confirmatory outcomes. Justify replication/power at the randomized and biological
levels. No numerical margin, sample size, power or cost is currently established.

Report effect sizes and uncertainty. Use clone/day and missing-outcome sensitivity
analyses; bounds do not replace sampling uncertainty. Prespecify an endpoint family
and inferential strategy. Broad model/drug/endpoint searches and repeated looks cannot
be retrospectively described as one confirmatory test. No post-hoc branch switching,
control removal or threshold relaxation is permitted in that test.

For oriented benefit Δ and justified minimum δ, an interval entirely above δ supports
the scoped meaningful-benefit requirement. An interval entirely below δ rejects that
tested meaningful-benefit claim, not every effect. An interval spanning δ is inconclusive.
For injury H and acceptable bound m, the upper interval limit must be below m to pass;
a nonsignificant difference from zero does not pass safety. A lower limit above m
triggers a safety stop. Equality at a bound stays a hold. A qualified observed safety
trigger stops advancement for review even if other gates remain unresolved.

## 7. Decision contract and public-compute calibration

The [decision contract](track2-decision-contract-v21.json) currently has 13 unknown
prerequisites, five unmeasured injury endpoints and null margins. The
[checker](../scripts/track2_falsification_v21.py) returns **HOLD**. A future laboratory
must substantiate gate adjudications and supply reviewed analysis; this software does
not estimate confidence intervals, verify experimental truth or authorize treatment.

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Failed assay/control or changed confirmatory plan | Invalid/hold inference; repair before another test. |
| Valid test rejects model/branch eligibility | Stop that branch; no retrospective substitution. |
| Missing measurement, unjustified margin or insufficient precision | Hold advancement. |
| Valid interval excludes the prespecified meaningful benefit | Stop that tested benefit claim. |
| Injury exceeds its bound or an observed safety trigger occurs | Stop advancement for safety review. |
| All scoped gates and benefit/injury requirements pass | Preclinical review only. |

Before more transcriptome ranking, qualify a filter on independent known-positive and
negative perturbations, with cell context and seed/potency/off-target structure matched
where possible. Lack of metadata is a limitation, not permission to assume matching.
Lock preprocessing and the comparison family; keep held-out validation independent of
candidate/HT29 selection. The v19 0/14 and post-hoc HT29 tail 0.042 remain unchanged.
No false-negative rate, sensitivity or calibrated probability follows from them. Low
drug activity is compatible with a weak biological response or an inadequate assay;
it does not establish efficacy or harm. Additional GPU volume cannot resolve this.
