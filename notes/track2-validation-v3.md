# Track 2 v3: additional falsification gates

2026-09-09. Read with the preserved [base design](track2-validation.md) and
[scientific supplement](track2-firecrawl-scientific-review.md). This is a proposed
design, not performed work, dosing guidance or permission to obtain patient material.
Trans phase remains unconfirmed; clinical exposure margins remain unknown.

## 1. Measure fates, not just the surviving mitoses

The new rapamycin study changes the required readout, not the inferred clinical
effect. It tested nocodazole-arrested A549 cancer cells, not everolimus in deficient
normal cells. Its slippage denominator excludes cells outside the death/slippage
comparison. Do not transplant that percentage into an MVA efficacy estimate.
[Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2021.103675)

Before observing treatment outcomes, define enrollment, follow-up duration, tracking
loss, exclusion criteria and mutually exclusive first-event categories. Track cells
from before exposure, retaining arrest and censored tracks. Distinguish accurate
division, error-containing division, mitotic death, slippage without division and
persistent arrest. Follow daughters and post-slippage cells for survival and regrowth.
Repeated divisions require a stated per-division denominator; they are not extra
independent clones. A blinded reviewer should check ambiguous events.

Report fate counts against the enrolled population and time at risk, not only a
selected endpoint subset. Analyse cells nested within treatment wells, clone and day;
predefine the confirmatory estimand after a pilot establishes assay feasibility and
variance. Track loss cannot automatically be classified as cell death. Stratify
genotype and tissue comparisons rather than pooling unequal observation windows.

Stop the constitutional-rescue arm if apparent improvement reflects inhibited entry
into mitosis, selective death, slippage or subsequent expansion of abnormal survivors.
Require independent attachment/segregation and tissue-function measurements. A smaller
percentage of abnormal mitoses alone cannot pass the gate. Corrected, deficient-normal
and appropriate tumour controls remain essential.

## 2. Separate potency, regression and durability

The new RD compound study disagrees across SRB potency and colony suppression;
pralatrexate models show regression followed by recurrence. These are not interchangeable
endpoints. Pair short-term biomass/ATP measurements with direct cell counts, cell-fate
assessment and validated post-treatment regrowth/clonogenic assays. Distinguish no
growth during exposure from irreversible killing, while recognising that reversible
on-treatment benefit can still be real. Do not call recurrence a cure.
[RD study](https://doi.org/10.1002/ddr.70304),
[FP-RMS study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73749-y)

Culture seeding/recovery conditions, washout, solvent controls, initial viable-cell
counts and assay interference need prespecification. SRB, ATP and LC3 signals are not
independent proof of cell killing or autophagy-dependent death. The existing flux and
genetic-perturbation controls remain necessary.

## 3. Pralatrexate is an optional tumour-only branch

Do not activate this branch by assuming a fusion-positive tumour. Verify the proposed
experimental model's molecular subtype; this does not establish the subject's unknown
tumour fusion status. Require oncology-reviewer comparator selection and separate
single-agent controls before considering a combination. Concurrent and alternating
schedules cannot be pooled, especially where toxicity changed scheduling or terminated
treatment. Include deficient-normal renal/muscle and marrow-relevant safety evaluation
where the authorized laboratory can justify and qualify those models.

The existing exposure gate applies independently: nominal medium concentration,
mouse dose and human free tissue exposure are different quantities. No matched
functional-response/normal-injury exposure pair has been measured. The US PTCL label
does not establish pediatric RMS efficacy or safety. Do not make a clinical dose or
supplementation recommendation from this research plan.
[Study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73749-y),
[official label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=cc3df863-c51c-49c5-283a-d6808fb49258&version=6)

## 4. Keep intervention identity and evidence independence explicit

Rapamycin used as an engineered dimerizer does not test therapeutic mTOR rescue.
Require a matched tool/control design and direct functional endpoints before making
mechanism claims. A SIRT2 transgene is not a drug; its different-model negative result
does not refute a hypomorph result. Statin-induced tumour damage is not muscle rescue.
The supplement records these distinctions and corresponding primary citations.

Atlas AVI and merged-splicing results guide uncertainties to test, not drug selection.
Do not use low predicted splicing magnitudes to remove endogenous RNA/protein assays,
assume intact missense function, or claim absence of transcript decay. The cis/trans
engineering controls remain hypothetical models; only independent subject evidence
could resolve the currently unconfirmed phase.
