## Description to accompany the video

Rare Disease, Real Kid: The MVA Hackathon 2026 — Track 2, participant jvv7.

We propose a model-qualified test of everolimus, an existing approved mTORC1 inhibitor,
for downstream tissue function in BUB1B-associated MVA. This is a hypothesis for further
investigation, not evidence of efficacy or a treatment recommendation. No drug earns
rescue priority; HCQ remains reserve. Trans phase and clinical exposure margins are
unresolved, and no wet-lab experiment has been performed.
