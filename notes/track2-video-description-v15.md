## Description to accompany the video

Rare Disease, Real Kid: The MVA Hackathon 2026 — Track 2, participant jvv7.

We propose a model-qualified test of everolimus, an existing approved mTORC1 inhibitor,
for downstream tissue function in BUB1B-associated MVA. This is a hypothesis for further
investigation, not evidence of efficacy or a treatment recommendation. No drug earns
rescue priority; HCQ remains reserve. Trans phase and clinical exposure margins are
unresolved, and no wet-lab experiment has been performed.

The v15 falsification audit retains the original model-control success alongside
expanded-control failures and an unresolved primary-source concentration discrepancy.
Nineteen claim-specific falsifiers and stricter benefit/harm/exposure gates guide further
research. Passing software tests is not biological validation.

not on-demand inference; applicable output terms remain relevant, and no broader

settings remain unverified. Self-hosting does not imply local model inference or

Owner-hosted inference used ESM-1v/ESM-2, ESMC 300M/600M/6B, ESM3-open 1.4B,
Boltz-2, AlphaFold2, AlphaFold3, ESMFold2 and Evo2 7B/20B/40B.
Only public references, published controls and permitted derived candidate
substitutions entered those runs. ColabFold alignment search received only the
public wild-type protein domain; no training/retention policy is asserted for
that external service. Newer sequence models pass the small fixed control challenge
and strengthen computational motivation to test N1002K. Earlier disagreements and
failed controls remain relevant; confident structures do not establish function or
drug rescue. The newer runs reused the alignment without another external request.

The workflow uses public literature and permitted derived findings; raw subject
records and clinical narrative were not transmitted. Slides were rendered locally.
