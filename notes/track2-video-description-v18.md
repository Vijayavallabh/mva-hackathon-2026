## Description to accompany the video

Rare Disease, Real Kid: The MVA Hackathon 2026 · Track 2 · jvv7.

Can everolimus improve useful cell function in BUB1B-associated MVA? We propose a
qualified test of this approved mTORC1 inhibitor, with genotype-aware controls,
complete cell-fate accounting, independent confirmation and explicit stopping rules.
The mechanism is conditional; no drug earns rescue priority, no wet-lab experiment
has been performed, and phase, endogenous effects and clinical exposure remain unresolved.
This is a hypothesis for investigation, not a treatment recommendation.
