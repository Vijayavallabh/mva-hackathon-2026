# Track 2 v42: video description

Qualifying endogenous BUB1B function before advancing a drug hypothesis.
Everolimus is a conditional worked example on HOLD for missing prerequisites,
with proposed functional testing and explicit stopping rules. Everolimus remains an optional probe; no rescue-priority drug is supported.

Public RPE1/K562 data add a qualification test: BUB1B expression changes are
detectable, but weak own-target retrieval, one shared guide pair and selected controls
leave attribution unresolved. Independent restoration and relevant function are required.

The corrected imaging analysis gives BUB1B full-profile interphase ranks 1, 3, 3
and 2 among 994 genes. Neither arm passes the practical neural-gain gate. Mitotic
BUB1B retrieval is missing for coverage. Sampling, median and post-hoc plate controls
remain explicit; transformed HeLa images do not establish non-cancer rescue.

The three-minute schedule is a rehearsal plan; no recording, hosting or measured
runtime is established.

QC limits. MCF7 BUB1B reversal falls from rank 7 to 1,184; MTOR stays first. Different cells do not establish a
joint rescue result; reaggregated wells do not establish independent replication.
The human/animal findings are indirect for everolimus in MVA. Invalid assays and
imprecise results cannot establish biological failure or safety. Subject phase and
clinical exposure margins remain unknown. Tumour killing and non-cancer rescue need
separate tests. The report contains additional primary sources and limitations.
