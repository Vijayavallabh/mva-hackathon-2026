# Track 2 v60: three-minute pitch description

**Title:** Everolimus, conditionally: BUB1B mechanism to a testable drug hypothesis | MVA Hackathon 2026

Can an existing medicine help in BUB1B-associated mosaic variegated aneuploidy? This three-minute pitch proposes everolimus as a conditional approved-drug hypothesis for further investigation—not a treatment recommendation.

We characterize the retained BUB1B p.Leu737Ter / p.Asn1002Lys pair at gene level: BUBR1 contributes to spindle-checkpoint and chromosome-attachment control, and biallelic disruption is an established MVA mechanism. The selected pair’s endogenous effect, loss-of-function/gain-of-function direction and phase remain unmeasured, so the first experiment is model qualification rather than drug exposure.

The proposed decision-changing experiment is a four-arm comparison in an endogenously qualified model: deficient + vehicle, deficient + everolimus, corrected + vehicle, corrected + everolimus. Useful output, injury and recovery are measured together, with every founder cell enrolled before exposure. The framework separates a better surviving-cell ratio from genuinely useful cellular output: a synthetic example shows an error ratio improving from 25% to 11.1% while accurate viable output falls from 60/100 to 40/100.

No wet-lab experiment has been performed, no rescue-priority drug is claimed, and no clinical recommendation is made. Any care decision belongs to the child’s family and clinical team.
