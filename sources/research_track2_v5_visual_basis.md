- [Sieben et al., JCI](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/126863),
  DOI [10.1172/JCI126863](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI126863).
  Publisher page reopened. The earlier selected-section audit supports the
  allele-specific muscle mTOR observation and no rapalog-rescue claim.
  Keep [corrigendum](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI144781) with the report citation.
- [Gupta et al., ARST1431 primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11550893/),
  DOI [10.1016/S1470-2045(24)00255-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(24)00255-9).
  Indexed primary findings confirm 297 evaluable participants and no demonstrated
  three-year EFS benefit from adding temsirolimus (HR 0.86, CI 0.58-1.26, p=0.44).
  This tumour trial does not test constitutional everolimus rescue. Search also
  returned secondary commentary; that was not used as claim support.
- The fate tree is a proposed assay design from `notes/track2-validation-v4.md`,
  informed by [Yamada](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2021.103675). It is neither
  observed subject data nor an assertion that each trajectory is equally frequent.
- The candidate map retains v4 decisions. The pralatrexate boundary is supported
  by the archived [fusion-positive RMS study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73749-y).
  The map has no potency axis, effect sizes or numerical ranking.
